The **moss stitch** (also called linen stitch or granite stitch) gives crochet a tight, woven look that's both modern and classic. This **moss stitch crochet cowl pattern** is a relaxing, repetitive project – ideal if you want to learn a new stitch without any stress.

You only need two stitches: the chain and the single crochet.

## Why You'll Love This Crochet Cowl

- **Just two stitches** – chain and single crochet.
- **Beautiful woven texture** that doesn't curl.
- **No seams** – it's worked in the round.
- **Easy to wear** – no ends flapping in the wind.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)

- **ch** – chain
- **sl st** – slip stitch
- **sc** – single crochet
- **ch-1 sp** – the space under a ch 1.

## Size and Gauge

- **Finished size:** about 60 cm around × 25 cm tall.
- **Gauge:** 16 stitches (sc and ch together) and 18 rounds = 10 cm.

## How to Crochet the Moss Stitch

Moss stitch is just **(sc, ch 1)** repeated. On each new round, you work your sc **into the ch-1 space** below, not into the sc. This staggers the stitches like bricks.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Foundation

Ch 120. Lay the chain flat and make sure it's **not twisted**. Join with a sl st into the first chain to make a ring.

### Round 1

Ch 1, *sc in the next ch, ch 1, skip 1 ch.* Repeat from * around. Join with a sl st to the first sc. **(60 sc and 60 ch-1 spaces)**

### Round 2

Sl st into the first ch-1 space. Ch 1, *sc in the ch-1 space, ch 1, skip the next sc.* Repeat from * around. Join.

### Repeat

Repeat Round 2 until the cowl measures **25 cm** tall – about 45 rounds.

### Edging

Work 1 round of sc along the top edge (1 sc in each sc and each ch-1 space). Fasten off. Join yarn on the bottom edge and repeat. Weave in all ends.

> **Beginner tip:** worried about twisting the chain? Work the cowl flat instead, in rows, turning at the end of each row. When it's 60 cm long, sew the short ends together.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)

- **My cowl has a twist in it.** The foundation chain twisted when joining. Unfortunately you'll need to start again – or make it an infinity scarf and call it a feature!
- **The texture looks like columns, not bricks.** Make sure you're working into the ch-1 space, not the sc.
- **The cowl is too tight.** Use a larger hook for the foundation chain only.

## How to Care for Your Cowl

Hand wash in cool water, gently squeeze out the water (don't wring) and dry flat.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between moss stitch and linen stitch in crochet?

They're the same stitch! It's also called granite stitch or woven stitch. It's a pattern of (sc, ch 1) with each sc worked into the space below.

### Is moss stitch good for beginners?

Yes. It uses only chains and single crochet, and the repeat is just two steps.

### How much yarn do I need for a crochet cowl?

About 200 m of aran weight yarn for this cowl. Moss stitch uses a little more yarn than plain rows because it's so dense.
