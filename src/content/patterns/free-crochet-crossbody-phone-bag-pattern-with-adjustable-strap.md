This pattern is great for beginners and experienced crocheters alike. You'll learn how to work both the bag and an adjustable strap, giving you confidence in your crochet skills. Plus, it's a delightful way to showcase your favorite colors in a practical item!

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern uses basic stitches, and the steps are clearly laid out. You’ll be guided through the process of making a handy phone bag step by step, so don't worry if you're new to crocheting.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- BLO – back loop only

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished dimensions**: 18 × 14 cm (7 × 5.5 in)
- **Strap dimensions**: Adjustable from 76 cm to 152 cm (30 in to 60 in)
- **Gauge**: 12 dc and 7 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in double crochet.

To ensure your bag turns out the right size, it's important to check your gauge. If your stitches are too tight or too loose, adjust your hook size accordingly.

## Step 1: Main Bag Body
The main bag body is worked in rows.  
1. **Foundation Chain**: Ch 30.
2. **Row 1**: Dc in 4th ch from hook (this counts as your first dc), dc across (28 sts).
3. **Rows 2–10**: Ch 3 (counts as 1st dc), turn, dc in each st across (28 sts).
4. **Row 11**: Ch 1, turn, sc in BLO in each st across (28 sts).
5. **Row 12**: Ch 3, turn, dc in each st across (28 sts).
6. **Row 13**: Ch 1, turn, sc in each st across (28 sts).

## Step 2: Flap
The flap is worked directly on top of the main bag body.  
7. **Row 14**: Ch 1, turn, sc in each st across (28 sts).
8. **Row 15**: Ch 1, turn, sc in each st across (28 sts).

## Step 3: Edging
Add a nice finishing touch with edging.  
9. **Edging**: Work 1 round of sc around the entire bag. Place 3 sc in each corner. Join with sl st to the first sc.

## Step 4: Adjustable Strap
This strap provides versatility for wearing.  
1. **Foundation Chain**: Ch 6.
2. **Row 1**: Sc in 2nd ch from hook, sc across (5 sts).
3. **Rows 2–55**: Ch 1, turn, sc in each st across (5 sts).
4. **Finishing the Strap**: Fold the strap in half and sew together with sc along the long edges, creating a tube.

## Assembly & Finishing
- **Attach Strap**: Use the tapestry needle to securely sew both ends of the strap to the top corners of the bag.
- **Button**: Sew the button to the front flap, making sure it aligns with the bag’s body for closure.
- **Optional Lining**: If using felt lining, cut it to size and hand-stitch or glue it inside the bag for added durability. 
- Weave in all ends securely.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Crossbody Phone Bag
- Keep your workspace organized, so you can find materials easily.
- Pay attention to your tension for even stitches.
- Measure dimensions as you go to ensure they match the pattern.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My bag is too small." – This might be due to incorrect gauge. Check your gauge before starting and adjust your hook size if needed.
- "The stitches are uneven." – This can happen from varying tension. Focus on maintaining consistent tension throughout your project.
- "The strap feels too flimsy." – Make sure you’re not skipping stitches or not working enough rows. More rows will make the strap sturdier.

## How to Care for Your Crossbody Phone Bag
For best results, hand wash your bag in cold water and lay it flat to dry. This will help it maintain its shape and color.
