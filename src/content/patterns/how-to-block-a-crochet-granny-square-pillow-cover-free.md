This crochet granny square pillow cover features a classic design made up of colorful granny squares. Once completed, the pillow cover is blocked for a crisp finish, giving it a polished look. You'll find this project enjoyable and straightforward, using basic stitches like double crochet and slip stitch. 

This pattern is perfect for intermediate crocheters ready to create a beautiful pillow cover that will enhance any room. You’ll learn how to make granny squares and assemble them into a lovely finished product.

## Skill Level: Intermediate
You'll need to know how to make chains, slip stitches, and double crochet stitches. You’ll also be introduced to the granny square technique.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sl st – slip stitch
- dc – double crochet
- sp – space
- rnd – round
- st(s) – stitch(es)

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished Pillow Cover Dimensions:** 40 × 40 cm (16 × 16 in)
- **Gauge:** 4 squares = 20.3 × 20.3 cm (8 × 8 in).

To ensure your squares are the correct size, make sure to check your gauge. Adjust your hook size if necessary.

## Step 1: Making the Granny Squares
The granny squares are made in rounds. You’ll need to make 16 squares.

### Square 1 (Using Color A):
- Make a magic ring. 
- Ch 3, (2 dc, ch 1) x 3, join (4 groups) (4 sts).
- Rnd 2: Sl st to next ch 1 sp, ch 3, (2 dc, ch 1, 3 dc) in same sp, *[(3 dc, ch 1) in next ch 1 sp]* x 3, join (12 sts).
- Rnd 3: Sl st to next ch 1 sp, ch 3, (2 dc, ch 1, 3 dc) in same sp, *[(3 dc, ch 1) in next ch 1 sp]* x 3, join (20 sts).
- Rnd 4: Sl st to next ch 1 sp, ch 3, (2 dc, ch 1, 3 dc) in same sp, *[(3 dc, ch 1) in next ch 1 sp]* x 3, join (28 sts).

### Repeat for Squares 2 to 16:
Change colors using the following sequence: A, B, C, D, A, B, C, D, A, B, C, D, A, B, C, D. Each square will finish with 28 stitches.

## Step 2: Joining Squares
Now, we’ll assemble the squares into a pillow cover. 

- Lay the 16 squares in a 4 × 4 formation.
- Using a slip stitch, join the squares side by side.
- Join the rows first, then connect the rows.

## Step 3: Edging
With the final color of your choice (Color E):
- Work a round of single crochets evenly around the perimeter of the pillow cover (approximately 60 stitches).
- Fasten off and weave in any ends.

## Step 4: Blocking
To finish the pillow cover:
- Use a wet cloth or steam to dampen the cover.
- Lay the cover flat on blocking mats and shape it to 40 × 40 cm (16 × 16 in).
- Pin into place, ensuring the corners are square.
- Allow it to dry completely.

## Step 5: Inserting Pillow
Once blocked, insert your pillow form into the cover. Adjust for fullness as necessary.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Granny Square Pillow Cover
- Keep your yarn organized to avoid tangles.
- Maintain consistent tension throughout your work for even squares.
- Check your gauge periodically as you make your squares.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My squares aren’t the same size." – This may be due to inconsistent tension. Keep your tension even and check your gauge.
- "The squares look wobbly." – This can happen if you start your rounds too tightly. Loosen your grip to create a more uniform square.
- "The edges aren’t straight." – Ensure you pin your cover nicely while blocking. This helps maintain shape and crisp edges.

## How to Care for Your Granny Square Pillow Cover
Hand wash your pillow cover in cool water with mild detergent. Lay it flat to dry and avoid direct sunlight to prevent color fading.
