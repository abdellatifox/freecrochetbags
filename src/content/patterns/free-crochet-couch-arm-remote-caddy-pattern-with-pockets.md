This crochet couch arm remote caddy pattern makes a practical and chic holder for your remotes and small items. It's easy to create with simple stitches and pockets for easy access to your essentials. The project is not only fun but also a great way to enhance your living space.

This pattern is well-suited for anyone looking to tidy up their couch or armchair. You'll learn the basics of double crochet and how to assemble pockets, making it perfect for beginners ready to take on a small project.

## Why You'll Love This Couch Arm Remote Caddy
- Super functional – keeps your remotes and snacks organized and within reach.
- Simple construction – just basic stitches and easy assembly makes it enjoyable to crochet.
- Customizable colors – match it to your home decor or express your personality with different yarns.
- Quick to make – you can create this caddy in just a couple of hours, making it a perfect project for a rainy day.

## Skill Level: Easy
To make this caddy, you should know how to chain (ch), double crochet (dc), slip stitch (sl st), and single crochet (sc). You'll be learning how to create pockets, which adds a fun twist to the project!

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- dc – double crochet
- sc – single crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- sp – space

## Size and Gauge
- Finished size: 30 × 20 cm (12 × 8 in)
- Gauge: 12 dc and 7 rows = 10 cm (4 in)
- To achieve the correct gauge, make a swatch and adjust your hook size if necessary.

## Step 1: Making the Main Body
The main body is worked in rows.   
1. Foundation: Ch 40. (40 sts)  
2. Row 1: Dc in 4th ch from hook, dc in each ch across. Turn. (38 dc)  
3. Row 2: Ch 3 (counts as first dc), dc in each dc across. Turn. (38 dc)  
4. Row 3 to Row 14: Repeat Row 2 for a total of 13 more rows. (38 dc each row)  
5. Row 15: Ch 1, sc in each dc across. Turn. (38 sc)  
6. Row 16: Ch 1, sc in first st, *ch 1, skip 1 st, sc in next st* repeat to end. Turn. (19 sc, 19 ch-1 spaces)  

## Step 2: Making the Pockets (Make 2)
The pockets are also worked in rows.  
1. Foundation: Ch 20. (20 sts)  
2. Row 1: Dc in 4th ch from hook, dc in each ch across. Turn. (18 dc)  
3. Row 2 to Row 5: Ch 3, dc in each dc across. Turn. (18 dc each row)  
4. Row 6: Ch 1, sc in each dc across. Turn. (18 sc)  

## Step 3: Assembly
We’ll put everything together now.  
1. Attach Pockets: Position the pockets on the front of the main body, centered, and pin down. Sew the two pockets securely using slip stitches along the sides and bottom.  
2. Finishing the Caddy: Fold the main body in half lengthwise, with the wrong sides together. Slip stitch along the sides, leaving the top open.  

## Finishing Tip: Weave Ends Neatly
After completing the caddy, make sure to fasten off all your yarn ends and weave them in for a clean finish.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Couch Arm Remote Caddy
- Stay organized – keep your yarn and tools in one place to avoid losing anything while you crochet.
- Keep tension even – maintaining the same tension will help ensure your stitches look consistent.
- Check size as you go – occasionally measure your work against the finished sizes to ensure accuracy.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My caddy is too small. – The foundation chain may be too short. Just increase the number of chains in the foundation.
- The stitches are uneven. – This can happen if your tension varies. Focus on keeping a consistent grip as you work.
- The pockets are floppy. – If they aren’t sturdy, consider using a firmer yarn or a lining to reinforce them.

## How to Care for Your Couch Arm Remote Caddy
To keep your caddy looking its best, machine wash it in cold water and lay it flat to dry. If you've added fabric linings, wash according to the fabric care instructions.

### FAQ
### Can I customize the size?
Yes! You can increase or decrease the foundation chain to make a larger or smaller caddy, just keep the overall proportions in mind.

### What yarn can I use instead of worsted weight?
You can use any medium weight yarn, but be sure to check your gauge so the final size is correct.

### How long will it take to make?
This crochet caddy takes approximately 2.5 hours to make, with around an hour and a half for the main body and 30 minutes for each pocket.
