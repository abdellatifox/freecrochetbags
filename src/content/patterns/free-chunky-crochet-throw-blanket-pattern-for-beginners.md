## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This blanket uses only a few basic stitches: the chain and double crochet. Plus, you'll be trying out the fun V-stitch, which gives the blanket its beautiful texture.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- dc – double crochet
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- sl st – slip stitch
- sp – space
- V-Stitch – (dc, ch 1, dc) in the same stitch

## Gauge and Finished Size
- Final dimensions: 127 × 152 cm (50 × 60 in)
- Gauge: 8 dc and 5 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in the main stitch pattern

Check your gauge by making a small swatch. Make sure to adjust your hook size as needed to match the gauge, ensuring your blanket turns out the right size.

## Step 1: Body of the Blanket
The body of the blanket is worked in rows. 

1. **Foundation Chain:** Chain 72 (or desired width in multiples of 3).
2. **Row 1:** Dc in the 4th ch from the hook, dc in each ch across (70 dc).
3. **Row 2:** Ch 3 (counts as 1st dc), turn. V-stitch in the next st, *skip 2 sts, v-stitch in next st*; repeat from * across, dc in last st (24 v-sts and 1 dc).
4. **Row 3:** Ch 3, turn. V-stitch in first v-st, *skip next v-st, v-stitch in next v-st*; repeat from * across, dc in top of turning chain (24 v-sts and 1 dc).
5. **Rows 4–10:** Repeat Row 3 for 7 more rows (total of 10 rows).
6. **Color Change:** Change to Color B at the end of Row 10.
7. **Rows 11–20:** With Color B, repeat Rows 2–10.
8. **Row 21:** Change back to Color A and repeat Rows 2–10.
9. Continue alternating between Color A and Color B for a total of 20 rows until the blanket measures approximately 152 cm (60 in) in length, finishing with Color A.
10. **Final Row:** For the last row, ch 3, dc in each st across (70 dc).

## Optional Edging
For a polished finish, you may add an optional single crochet edge around the entire blanket.

## Finishing Tip: Weave Ends Neatly
Weave in all loose ends using the tapestry needle, and block the blanket lightly to expand the stitches if needed.

## Beginner Tips
- Keep your workspace organized – having everything at hand will help you focus on your stitches.
- Maintain even tension – consistent tension leads to a neater finish.
- Check your size as you go – ensure your blanket is measuring out correctly.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My blanket feels uneven." – This might be from inconsistent tension. To fix it, practice on a small swatch before starting the blanket.
- "The stitches are too tight." – Check that you're using the right hook size; a larger hook can help loosen your tension.
- "I lost count of my stitches." – Keep a stitch marker handy to mark your starting point for each row so you can easily track your progress.

## How to Care for Your Chunky Crochet Blanket
Machine wash on a gentle cycle and lay it flat to dry to maintain its cozy softness. Avoid bleach to keep the colors vibrant.

### FAQ
### Can I customize the size?
Absolutely! To make a larger blanket, simply add more chains in multiples of 3 for the foundation chain and extend the number of rows to your desired length.

### What if I can't find the exact yarn?
You can substitute with any bulky-weight yarn! Just make sure it has similar thickness and fiber content to achieve the same cozy feel.

### How long will it take to complete?
Most find it takes about 10–15 hours, depending on personal skill and speed. Take your time and enjoy the process!
