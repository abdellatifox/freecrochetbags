## Why You'll Love This Market Bag

- **One piece** – no sewing panels together.
- **Stretchy mesh** – holds more than it looks like it can.
- **Cotton yarn** – strong, washable and easy to find.

![Finished crochet market bag with mesh body, laid flat](/images/patterns/example-contentops-pattern-2.jpg)

## How to Crochet the Base

Ch 41. Sc in the second chain from the hook and in each chain across (40 sc). Work in rounds around both sides of the chain.

### Round 1

Ch 1, work 2 sc in the first stitch, sc around, placing 3 sc in each end. Join with a sl st.

## FAQ

### Can a beginner make this bag?

Yes. If you can chain, single crochet and double crochet, you can make this bag.

### What yarn is best for a market bag?

Cotton or a cotton blend: it is strong and does not stretch out of shape when the bag is full.
