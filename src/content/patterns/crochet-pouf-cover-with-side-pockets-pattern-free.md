This crochet pouf cover pattern makes a stylish and functional addition to your living space. The Pocket Pouf features convenient side pockets, making it perfect for storing small items like remotes or magazines. Using basic double crochet and single crochet stitches, this project is both enjoyable and approachable. 

Ideal for intermediate crocheters, you’ll learn techniques for making pockets and working in the round, enhancing your crafting skills.

## Why You'll Love This Pocket Pouf
- Stylish and functional – adds a charming touch to any room while offering storage.
- Customizable design – use different colors to make it personal and unique.
- Great for practice – work on your double crochet and pocket-making skills.
- Practical project – perfect for creating a cozy atmosphere and keeping your space tidy.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! While this pattern is categorized as intermediate, the stitches used are basic. You’ll have the opportunity to practice simple techniques, making it a great step up from basic projects.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- st – stitch
- dc – double crochet
- sc – single crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- sp – space
- tr – treble crochet
- rep – repeat

## Size and Gauge
- Finished size: approximately 46 cm diameter and 36 cm height (18 × 14 in).
- Pocket dimensions: approximately 20 cm wide and 15 cm tall (8 × 6 in).
- Gauge: 12 dc and 7 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in double crochet. Check your gauge, as adjustments in hook size may be necessary.

## Step 1: Start with the Base
The base of the pouf cover is worked in rounds. 
- Round 1: Ch 4, join to form a ring. Ch 3 (counts as 1 dc), 11 dc in the ring. Join with sl st to the top of the ch 3. (12 dc)
- Round 2: Ch 3, 1 dc in the same st, 2 dc in each st around. Join with sl st. (24 dc)
- Round 3: Ch 3, *1 dc in the next st, 2 dc in the next st,* rep from * to * around. Join with sl st. (36 dc)
- Round 4: Ch 3, *1 dc in the next 2 sts, 2 dc in the next st,* rep from * to * around. Join with sl st. (48 dc)
- Round 5: Ch 3, *1 dc in the next 3 sts, 2 dc in the next st,* rep from * to * around. Join with sl st. (60 dc)
- Round 6: Ch 3, *1 dc in the next 4 sts, 2 dc in the next st,* rep from * to * around. Join with sl st. (72 dc)

## Step 2: Continue Upwards
We will create the body of the pouf cover.
- Rounds 7–16: Ch 3, dc in each st around. (72 dc)
- Round 17: Change to Seafoam Green for the first round of the pockets. Ch 3, dc in each st around. (72 dc)
- Rounds 18–28: Continue with Natural. Ch 3, dc in each st around. (72 dc)

## Step 3: Create the Pockets
The pockets are made separately and attached later.
### Left Pocket
- Round 1: Attach Seafoam Green at the desired location, ch 1, sc in next 8 sts. (8 sc)
- Round 2: Ch 1, sc in each st around. (8 sc)
- Round 3: Ch 1, *sc, ch 2, sc in the same st; skip 1 st,* repeat around. (8 sc, 8 ch spaces)

### Right Pocket
- Round 1: Attach Seafoam Green opposite the left pocket, ch 1, sc in next 8 sts. (8 sc)
- Round 2: Repeat same as left pocket. (8 sc)
- Round 3: Repeat same as left pocket. (8 sc, 8 ch spaces)

## Finishing the Pockets
- Edging: For both pockets, sc in each space around for a clean edge.

## How to Join Your Pieces
- Weave in all ends using a tapestry needle.
- Join the ends of the pockets to the main circular part of the pouf cover using sl st.
- Block the pouf cover if necessary for even shape and sizing.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Pouf Cover
- Keep track of your stitch counts with stitch markers.
- Maintain an even tension to avoid puckering.
- Check your size regularly to ensure a properly fitted pouf.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My pouf is uneven." – This typically happens due to inconsistent tension. Practice keeping your tension steady as you work.
- "The pockets are smaller than expected." – This may be due to counting errors. Double-check your stitch counts in each round.
- "My edges are curling." – This can occur if your gauge is too tight. Consider using a larger hook for looser stitches.

## How to Care for Your Pocket Pouf
Hand wash in cold water and lay flat to dry. This will help maintain the integrity and appearance of your crochet pouf cover.
