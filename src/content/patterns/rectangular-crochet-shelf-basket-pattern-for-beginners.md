## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern uses basic stitches and straightforward techniques. You'll be guided through each step while building your confidence in crochet.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- dc – double crochet
- sc – single crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- st – stitch

## Size and Gauge
- Finished Size: 30 × 20 × 20 cm (12 × 8 × 8 in)
- Gauge: 12 dc and 7 rows = 10 cm (4 in). Make sure to measure your gauge to achieve the correct size.

## How to Crochet a Rectangular Shelf Basket (Step by Step)

### Base of Basket
The base is worked in rows.
1. **Foundation Chain:** Ch 34. 
2. **Row 1:** Dc in 4th ch from hook and in each ch across. (32 dc)
3. **Row 2:** Ch 3 (counts as first dc), turn, dc in each dc across. (32 dc)
4. **Row 3:** Ch 1, turn, sc in first st, *ch 1, skip next st, sc in next st; repeat from * across to last st, sc in last st. (16 sc, 16 ch-1 spaces)
5. **Rows 4–8:** Repeat Row 2. (Total of 8 rows)

### Sides of Basket
The sides are built upon the base.
6. **Row 9:** Ch 1, turn, sc in first st, *ch 1, skip next st, sc in next st; repeat from * across. (16 sc, 16 ch-1 spaces)
7. **Row 10:** Ch 3, turn, dc in each sc and ch-1 space across. (32 dc)
8. **Rows 11–18:** Repeat Rows 9–10 four more times. (Total of 9 rows side height)

### Handles
The handles are crocheted directly onto the sides.
9. **Row 19 (First Handle):** Ch 1, turn, sc in first 8 st. (8 sc)
10. **Row 20:** Ch 3, turn, dc in each sc across. (8 dc)
11. **Rows 21–23:** Repeat Row 20. (Total of 3 rows)

12. **Row 24:** Ch 1, turn, sc in each st across, at the end of the last row, continue with the next side. (8 sc)

13. **Rows 25–30:** Repeat Rows 9–18 for the opposite side.

14. **Row 31 (Second Handle):** Repeat Rows 19–23 for the second handle.

15. **Final Row:** Ch 1, sc around the entire top edge, working 2 sc in each corner to round it out. Join with a sl st to starting ch.

## Finishing Tip: Weave Ends Neatly
Weave in all loose ends using the tapestry needle, ensuring everything is secure and neat before using your basket.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Basket
- Keep your workspace organized – it's easier to find what you need.
- Maintain even tension throughout your work for a consistent look.
- Check your gauge before starting to ensure the right size.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My basket is too small." – This might be due to incorrect gauge; check your swatch and adjust your hook size if necessary.
- "The stitches are uneven." – This can happen from inconsistent tension; try practicing on a smaller swatch to find your rhythm.
- "I skipped stitches." – Keep track of your stitches and use stitch markers if necessary to stay on track.

## How to Care for Your Basket
To keep your basket looking great, machine wash it on a gentle cycle and lay it flat to dry. This will help maintain its shape and appearance.
