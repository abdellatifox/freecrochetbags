## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- dc – double crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- inc – increase (2 dc in the same stitch)

## Size and Gauge
- Finished Bag Size: 15 × 20 cm (6 × 8 in)
- Finished Strap Length: Adjustable from 102 to 132 cm (40 to 52 in)
- Gauge: 12 dc and 7 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in double crochet.

To ensure your bag matches the desired size, check your gauge before starting. Adjust your hook size if necessary to achieve the correct measurements.

## Step 1: Making the Bag Body
The bag body is worked in rows, starting with the foundation chain.
1. Foundation Row: Ch 30. (30 sts)
2. Row 1: Dc in 4th ch from hook (this counts as the first dc), dc in each ch across. (28 sts)
3. Row 2: Ch 3, turn. Dc in each st across. (28 sts)
4. Row 3–10: Repeat Row 2 until you have a total of 10 rows. (28 sts)
5. Row 11: Change to Contrasting Color, ch 3, turn. Dc in each st across. (28 sts)
6. Row 12: Ch 3, turn. Dc in each st across. (28 sts)
7. Row 13: Change back to Main Color, ch 1, turn. Sc in each st across. (28 sts)
8. Row 14–15: Ch 1, turn. Sc in each st across for 2 rows. (28 sts)

## Step 2: Adding the Side Edging
This step adds a finished look to the bag, creating a nice edge.
9. Row 16: Ch 1, turn. Sc down the side of the bag, 2 sc in the corner, sc across the bottom, 2 sc in the next corner, sc up the other side. Join with a sl st to the first sc. (Approximately 64 sts)
10. Row 17: Ch 1, turn. Sc in each sc around. Join. (64 sts)
11. Row 18: Repeat Row 17 for 2 more rounds. Fasten off and weave in ends.

## Step 3: Crocheting the Strap
The strap is worked separately and then attached to the bag.
12. Strap: Ch 100. (100 sts)
13. Row 1: Dc in each ch across. (100 sts)
14. Row 2: Ch 3, turn. Dc in each st across. (100 sts)
15. Row 3: Repeat Row 2 for a total of 10 rows. Fasten off and weave in ends.

## How to Assemble Your Bag
- Fold the bag in half with the wrong sides facing out and seam the side edges together using a slip stitch or sc, leaving the top open.
- Use a tapestry needle to attach the button to the front of the bag, centered below the top edge.
- Attach the D-rings to each side of the bag on the top edge, then thread the strap through the D-rings so it can adjust its length.

## Beginner Tips
- Keep your stitches consistent for a neater finish.
- Check your gauge before starting to ensure the right size.
- Use a stitch marker to help track your rounds.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My bag is too small. – This could be due to a tight tension or incorrect gauge. Check your gauge and adjust your hook size if needed.
- The stitches are uneven. – This often happens if you rush. Take your time and focus on your tension for a more consistent look.
- I’m confused about the strap length. – If it seems too short or long, adjust the chain number at the start to fit your needs.

## How to Care for Your Phone Crossbody Bag
Hand wash in cold water and lay it flat to dry. Avoid wringing or twisting the bag to keep its shape intact.

### FAQ
### Can I customize the size?
Absolutely! You can adjust the foundation chain to make a larger or smaller bag. Just remember to maintain the same stitch count across all rows.

### What if I want to use different yarn?
You can use any worsted weight yarn. Just be sure to check the gauge to keep the bag size consistent.

### How long will this take me to make?
The total estimated time for this bag is around 3 hours, with about 2 hours for the body and 1 hour for the strap.
