This crochet bag pattern makes a fun and functional Flip Flap Bag with interchangeable button-on flaps. The design allows you to customize your bag to match any outfit, and it uses basic stitches like double crochet and single crochet that are easy to follow. You'll enjoy crafting this bag while learning how to create buttonholes and assemble a structured piece.

This project is perfect for anyone who wants a stylish bag while exploring their crochet skills. With straightforward steps, you'll gain confidence as you work through the main body and flaps, making it a great choice for intermediate crocheters looking to advance their skills.

## Why You'll Love This Flip Flap Bag
- Customizable design – Easily switch out flaps to match your style or mood.
- Functional size – Perfect for everyday use, carrying essentials without being bulky.
- Quick and rewarding – Finish the bag in about 8–12 hours, so you can enjoy it soon!
- Great for gifting – A thoughtful handmade present for friends and family.

## Skill Level: Intermediate
For this project, you'll need to know basic stitches like chain (ch), slip stitch (sl st), single crochet (sc), and double crochet (dc). You’ll also learn how to make buttonholes, which is a handy skill!

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sl st – slip stitch
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- hdc – half double crochet
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- sp – space
- inc – increase (two stitches in one stitch)
- dec – decrease (crochet two stitches together)

## Size and Gauge
- Finished size: Approximately 30.5 × 25.4 × 10.2 cm (12 × 10 × 4 in)
- Gauge: 12 dc and 8 rows = 10 cm (4 in)

To achieve the correct gauge, make a swatch of 20 dc across and 16 rows. Adjust your hook if necessary to match.

## Step 1: Bag Body
The bag body is worked back and forth in rows. 
1. **Foundation**: Ch 40 (40 sts)
2. **Row 1**: dc in 4th ch from hook, dc in each ch across (38 dc).
3. **Row 2–20**: Ch 3 (counts as first dc), turn, dc in each st across (38 dc). Continue for 19 more rows until the desired height is reached.
4. **Row 21**: Ch 1, sc in each st across (38 sc).
5. **Row 22**: Ch 1, turn, sc in each st across (38 sc).
6. **Finish**: Fasten off and weave in ends.

## Step 2: Flap Design
You'll create two separate flaps, each in different colors.
### Flap A
1. **Foundation**: Ch 30 (30 sts).
2. **Row 1**: dc in 4th ch from hook, dc in each ch across (28 dc).
3. **Row 2–10**: Ch 3, turn, dc in each st across (28 dc).
4. **Row 11**: Ch 1, sc in each st across (28 sc).
5. **Fasten off**.

### Flap B
1. Use the same steps as Flap A, but with Teal yarn.

## Step 3: Button Holes
To create button holes for both flaps:
1. On the top edge of each flap: 
   - Row 1: Ch 1, sc in first sc, ch 2, skip next 2 sc, sc in next sc. Repeat across.
   - Row 2: Ch 1, sc in each st and space across.

## Assembly & Finishing
1. Align the bottom of the bag body to create the base.
2. Join the sides using slip stitches or single crochet to form the bag shape.
3. Sew the buttons onto the bag body where the flaps will attach.
4. If using, cut the lining fabric to fit inside the bag's interior and sew or baste it together.
5. If desired, block the bag to shape using a damp cloth.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Flip Flap Bag
- Keep your yarn and supplies organized to avoid confusion.
- Maintain even tension for a consistent finish.
- Check the size and fit of your flaps and buttonholes as you go.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My bag looks uneven." – This often happens if your tension changes. Check your tension and relax your grip while crocheting.
- "The buttonholes are too tight." – If the holes don’t fit the buttons well, make sure to chain the same number in your buttonholes. Adjust if necessary.
- "My bag is taller than expected." – This can happen if you lose track of the rows. Count your rows as you crochet to maintain the right height.

## How to Care for Your Flip Flap Bag
To keep your bag looking great, hand wash it in cold water and lay it flat to dry. Avoid machine washing, as this may affect the shape and feel of the cotton yarn.

## FAQ
### Can I customize the size?
Yes! You can adjust the foundation chain and rows to make the bag larger or smaller. Just remember to keep the proportions in mind.

### What type of yarn can I use?
You can use any worsted weight (Category 4) yarn, but keep in mind that different yarns may alter the bag's structure and drape.

### How long will it take to make this bag?
The estimated time ranges from 8 to 12 hours, depending on your crocheting speed and experience level.
