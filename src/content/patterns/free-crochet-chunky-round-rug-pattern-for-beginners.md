This crochet chunky round rug pattern makes a wonderful addition to your living space with its soft t-shirt yarn and eye-catching design. It's enjoyable to work up and perfect for beginners to intermediate crocheters as it uses simple stitches with a bit of increasing technique. 

With this project, you'll learn how to crochet in rounds, how to make a magic ring, and you can customize the size of your rug easily.

- Stylish Decor – Adds a chic, cozy element to your home.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern is easy to understand, with clear instructions for each round. You'll get the hang of the magic ring and crocheting in rounds with practice.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sl st – slip stitch
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- rnd – round
- st(s) – stitch(es)

## How to Make a Magic Ring
1. Wrap the yarn around your fingers to create a loop.
2. Insert your hook into the loop and pull up a loop.
3. Chain 1 to secure and start crocheting in the ring.

## Gauge and Finished Size
- Gauge: 6 dc and 6 rows = 10 cm (4 in)
- Finished Size: Approximately 90 cm (36 in) in diameter

## Step 1: Foundation
The foundation starts with a magic ring.
1. **Magic Ring**: Create a magic ring to start.
2. **Round 1**: Ch 3 (counts as first dc), work 11 dc into the ring. Pull the ring tight. Join with a sl st to the top of the beginning ch. (12 sts)

## Step 2: Building Rounds
Now we'll build up the rug with rounds of double crochet.
3. **Round 2**: Ch 3, dc in the same st, 2 dc in each st around. Join with sl st to top of beginning ch. (24 sts)
4. **Round 3**: Ch 3, dc in the same st, *dc in next st, 2 dc in next st; repeat from * around. Join with sl st. (36 sts)
5. **Round 4**: Ch 3, dc in the same st, *dc in next 2 sts, 2 dc in next st; repeat from * around. Join with sl st. (48 sts)
6. **Round 5**: Ch 3, dc in the same st, *dc in next 3 sts, 2 dc in next st; repeat from * around. Join with sl st. (60 sts)
7. **Round 6**: Ch 3, dc in the same st, *dc in next 4 sts, 2 dc in next st; repeat from * around. Join with sl st. (72 sts)

## Step 3: Final Rounds
In the final rounds, you'll keep the same stitch count.
8. **Round 7–12**: Ch 3, dc in each st around. Join with sl st. (72 sts each round)
   - At the end of Round 12, your rug should measure approximately 90 cm (36 in) in diameter.

## Finishing: Weave in Ends
- Fasten off after Round 12 and weave in all ends securely with a tapestry needle.
- If desired, apply fabric glue to the ends for additional security.
- Use scissors to trim any uneven pieces of yarn.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Rug
- Keep your yarn provided organized for easy access.
- Maintain consistent tension throughout for even stitches.
- Measure your rounds as you go to meet your desired size.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My rug is too small." – You might have missed rounds; double-check your counts and add more rounds.
- "The stitches are uneven." – This often happens with tension; practice to keep your yarn tight but not too tight.
- "I can't get the magic ring right." – It may seem tricky at first, but keep practicing! 

## How to Care for Your Rug
Hand wash gently in cold water and lay flat to dry. Avoid wringing out your rug to maintain its shape.

### FAQ

### What if I don't have t-shirt yarn?
You can use other chunky yarns, but ensure they’re heavy enough to maintain the rug's structure.
