## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! While this pattern is classified as intermediate, you'll find that with basic crochet skills like single crochet and working in rounds, you can tackle it with confidence. We'll take you through each step together, and before you know it, you'll have a beautiful laundry basket.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- R – round

## Gauge and Finished Size
- Finished dimensions: 40.5 cm (16 in) in diameter, 51 cm (20 in) tall.
- Gauge: 5 sts and 5 rounds = 10 cm (4 in) in single crochet in cotton cord. To ensure your basket turns out the right size, make a gauge swatch and adjust your hook size if needed.

## Step 1: Making the Base
The base is worked in rounds. 
1. Ch 4, sl st in first ch to form a ring. (1 st)
2. Ch 1, 8 sc in ring. (8 sts)
3. R2: 2 sc in each st around. (16 sts)
4. R3: *1 sc in next st, 2 sc in next st*; repeat from * to * around. (24 sts)
5. R4: *1 sc in next 2 sts, 2 sc in next st*; repeat from * to * around. (32 sts)
6. R5: *1 sc in next 3 sts, 2 sc in next st*; repeat from * to * around. (40 sts)
7. R6: *1 sc in next 4 sts, 2 sc in next st*; repeat from * to * around. (48 sts)
8. R7: *1 sc in next 5 sts, 2 sc in next st*; repeat from * to * around. (56 sts)

## Step 2: Crochet the Sides
The sides are also worked in rounds. 
9. R8: 1 sc in each st around. (56 sts)
10. R9: 1 sc in each st around. (56 sts)
11. R10: Change to Charcoal; 1 sc in each st around. (56 sts)
12. R11: 1 sc in each st around. (56 sts)
13. R12: 1 sc in each st around. (56 sts)
14. R13: Change back to Natural; 1 sc in each st around. (56 sts)
15. R14: 1 sc in each st around. (56 sts)
16. R15: Repeat R10 - R14 (3 times). You will have completed the sides.

## Step 3: Add Handles
Handles are worked into the top of the basket. 
17. R16: *Ch 15, sk next 10 sts, sc into next st*; repeat around (you will have 4 handle openings).
18. R17: In each ch space from the previous round, sc 6 to form handle. Repeat for each handle opening to form a total of 4 handles. 
19. Sl st in the first sc to finish. Fasten off.

## Finishing Tip: Weave Ends Neatly
Weave in all ends using a tapestry needle. If desired, apply fabric stiffener to the basket to enhance structure. Block the basket to shape using blocking mats.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Laundry Basket
- Keep your tension consistent – this will help the basket maintain its shape.
- Count your stitches regularly – it’s easy to lose track, especially in the beginning.
- Measure as you go – checking size at various stages helps ensure a good fit.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My basket is too small. – You may have crocheted too tightly or not checked gauge. Try adjusting your hook size next time.
- The stitches are uneven. – This can happen if your tension varies. Focus on maintaining the same tension throughout the project.
- The handles seem weak. – Ensure you followed all stitches in the handle properly. You can also add extra rounds if needed.

## How to Care for Your Laundry Basket
Hand wash your basket in cold water, and lay it flat to dry. Avoid wringing or twisting to maintain its shape and integrity.
