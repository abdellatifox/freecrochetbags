This free crochet kids mittens pattern makes a delightful pair of cozy mittens designed to keep little hands nice and warm. Featuring long ribbed cuffs, this pattern is perfect for beginners as it uses basic stitches and clear step-by-step instructions. You'll enjoy creating these mittens for your child or as a thoughtful gift.

This project is great for crocheters of all ages and allows you to learn how to make a simple yet stylish accessory. You'll discover how to crochet different parts, including the thumb hole and the ribbed cuffs, while working at an enjoyable pace.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern uses basic stitches like chain, double crochet, and single crochet. We’ve broken down the steps to ensure you can follow along easily.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- dc – double crochet
- st – stitch
- sl st – slip stitch
- rnd – round
- sc – single crochet

## Size and Gauge
- Finished Sizes: Small (15 cm / 6 in), Medium (18 cm / 7 in), Large (20 cm / 8 in) from the tip to the base of the cuff.
- Gauge: 14 dc and 8 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in double crochet. To check your gauge, make a 4-inch swatch. If it does not match, adjust your hook size.

## Step 1: Main Body of Mittens (Make 2)
The body of the mittens is worked in rows. 
1. With blue yarn and 5.5 mm (I/9) hook, ch 22.
2. Row 1: Dc in 4th ch from hook and in each ch across. (20 dc)
3. Row 2–12: Ch 3, turn, dc in each st across. (20 dc)
4. Row 13: Ch 1, turn, sc in each st across. (20 sc)
5. Fasten off.

## Step 2: Thumb Hole
We'll create a thumb hole now.
1. Attach yarn in the middle of one short edge of the body piece with a sl st.
2. Ch 1, sc across the next 10 stitches to create a foundation for the thumb.
3. Leave the next 5 stitches unworked for the hole.
4. Sc in the remaining 5 stitches. (20 total)
5. Row 1: Ch 1, turn, sc in each st across (20 sc).
6. Row 2: Repeat Row 1 (20 sc).
7. Fasten off, leaving a tail for seaming.

## Step 3: Ribbed Cuffs (Make 2)
The ribbed cuffs are made with white yarn. 
1. With white yarn and 4.5 mm (7) hook, ch 10.
2. Row 1: Sc in 2nd ch from hook and in each ch across. (9 sc)
3. Row 2: Ch 1, turn, *sc in the back loop only of the next st, sc in the front loop only of the next st; repeat from * across. 
4. Repeat Row 2 until the cuff measures approximately 6 inches (15 cm) long. 
5. Sl st to join the ends of the ribbing together. 

## Step 4: Assembly & Finishing
1. Turn the body of each mitten inside out. Align the cuff with the top edge of the mitten and pin in place.
2. Using the tails from the cuff, seam it to the mitten using a whip stitch or slip stitch. 
3. Flip the mittens to the right side and fold the cuffs down over the top of the mittens.
4. Weave in any remaining loose ends using a yarn needle for a clean finish.

## Beginner Tips for Neat Mittens
- Keep your yarn organized – use a container to hold your skeins while working.
- Maintain even tension – try to keep your stitches consistently tight or loose.
- Check size often – measure your mittens as you go to ensure they are coming out correctly.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My mittens feel too tight." – This might be due to tight stitching. Try using a larger hook or adjusting your tension.
- "The thumb hole is too small." – Ensure you've left enough stitches unworked for the hole; you can always adjust the number of skipped stitches.
- "My cuffs don’t look ribbed." – Check that you alternated between back loop and front loop single crochet correctly.

## How to Care for Your Mittens
Hand wash your mittens in cold water and lay them flat to dry. This will help maintain their shape and texture. Avoid wringing or twisting the mittens to prevent stretching.

## FAQ
### Can I customize the size? 
Yes! You can adjust the starting chains for smaller or larger sizes. Just make sure to keep the proportions consistent throughout the mitten.

### What yarn can I use instead? 
You can use any worsted weight yarn, but make sure to check the gauge to ensure a proper fit.

### How long will it take to complete? 
You can expect to spend around 4 hours in total, with about 1 hour per mitten and 30 minutes for each cuff.
