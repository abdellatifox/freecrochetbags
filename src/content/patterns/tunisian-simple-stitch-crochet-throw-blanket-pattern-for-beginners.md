## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! The Tunisian Simple Stitch is a great first step into Tunisian crochet. You'll learn to create a beautiful texture and gain confidence as you work through simple rows.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- TSS – Tunisian Simple Stitch: Insert hook into the next stitch, yarn over, pull up a loop.
- Return Pass – Yarn over, pull through one loop (to return), then yarn over and pull through two loops until one loop remains on the hook.

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished Size:** Approximately 127 × 152 cm (50 × 60 in).
- **Gauge:** 8 stitches and 6 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in TSS. Make a swatch if unsure and adjust your hook size if needed.

## How to Crochet the Blanket (Step by Step)
The body of the blanket is created using rows of the Tunisian Simple Stitch.

1. **Foundation Chain:**
   - With Color A, ch 60.

2. **Row 1:**
   - TSS in each ch across (60 loops on hook).

3. **Return Pass:**
   - Yarn over, pull through one loop, then yarn over and pull through two loops until one loop remains on the hook (you should have 1 loop left on hook).

4. **Row 2:**
   - With Color A, TSS in each vertical bar across (60 loops on hook).

5. **Return Pass:**
   - As described above.

6. **Rows 3-15:**
   - Repeat Rows 2 and 3 with Color A (60 loops on hook).

7. **Change Color:**
   - After completing Row 15, change to Color B to begin the next section.

8. **Rows 16-30:**
   - Repeat Rows 2 and 3 using Color B (60 loops on hook).

9. **Change Color:**
   - After completing Row 30, switch back to Color A.

10. **Rows 31-45:**
    - Repeat Rows 2 and 3 with Color A (60 loops on hook).

11. **Finish:**
    - Continue alternating colors as desired until the blanket reaches approximately 152 cm (60 in) or your preferred length. End with Color A.

12. **Last Return Pass:**
    - Complete the last row’s return pass.

## Finishing Tip: Weave Ends Neatly
- Fasten off the last loop and leave a tail for weaving in. Use the tapestry needle to weave in all ends securely.  
- Optionally, block the blanket gently to even out stitches and enhance the appearance.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Throw Blanket
- Stay organized by keeping your yarns separate.
- Maintain an even tension to ensure consistent stitches.
- Measure your work regularly to ensure you're on track with the size.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My stitches are too tight." – This could be caused by pulling the yarn too tightly during the return pass. Try relaxing your grip.
- "I'm missing some stitches." – Make sure to include the last vertical bar at the end of each row.
- "My blanket is not matching the gauge." – If your gauge is off, adjust your hook size to achieve the specified gauge.

## How to Care for Your Throw Blanket
Machine wash on a gentle cycle and lay flat to dry to maintain the shape and texture of your blanket. This helps keep it cozy for years to come.

### FAQ
### Can I customize the size?
Yes! You can adjust the foundation chain in multiples of 2 to make your blanket larger or smaller.

### Can I use a different yarn?
Absolutely! Just keep in mind that different yarn weights may affect the final size and gauge, so check your tension as you crochet.

### How long will it take to make?
It typically takes about 8–11 hours to complete, depending on your crochet speed and experience.
