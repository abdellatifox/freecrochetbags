This crochet vanity tray pattern makes a stylish and functional piece designed to help you organize and display small items like cosmetics or jewelry. Using sturdy cotton cord, this project features a clean, textured finish that enhances any decor style. 

It's a perfect project for beginners looking to learn basic crochet techniques while creating something useful.

## Why You'll Love This Vanity Tray
- Stylish and functional – organize your cosmetics and jewelry beautifully.
- Easy to customize – adjust the size to suit your space perfectly.
- Quick to make – complete your tray in about 2.5 hours.
- Suitable for all skill levels – perfect for both beginners and seasoned crocheters.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern uses basic stitches like single crochet and is straightforward to follow. With clear steps and a simple construction, you'll gain confidence as you create your own tray.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain  
- sc – single crochet  
- sl st – slip stitch  
- st(s) – stitch(es)  
- FO – finish off  
- BLO – back loop only

## Size and Gauge
- Finished Size: Approximately 31 cm (12 in) diameter and 7.6 cm (3 in) tall.
- Gauge: 6 sc and 6 rounds = 10 cm (4 in).
- To check your gauge, measure over a 10 cm (4 in) square. Adjust your hook size if necessary.

## Step 1: Making the Base
The base is worked in rounds.  
**Round 1:** Ch 4, join with a sl st to form a ring. (1 st)  
**Round 2:** Ch 1, work 6 sc into the ring. Join with a sl st to the first sc. (6 sts)  
**Round 3:** Ch 1, *1 sc in the next st, 2 sc in the next st*; repeat from * around. Join with a sl st to the first sc. (18 sts)  
**Round 4:** Ch 1, *1 sc in the next 2 sts, 2 sc in the next st*; repeat from * around. Join with a sl st to the first sc. (24 sts)  
**Round 5:** Ch 1, *1 sc in the next 3 sts, 2 sc in the next st*; repeat from * around. Join with a sl st to the first sc. (30 sts)  

## Step 2: Crochet the Sides
The sides are created by working in rounds.  
**Rounds 6–15:** Ch 1, sc in each st around for 10 rounds. (30 sts)  
- After completing round 15, your tray should be about 7.6 cm (3 in) high at the edge.

## Step 3: Finish the Edges
**Finishing Round:** Ch 1, sc in each st around using BLO for 1 round for a polished look. (30 sts)  
**Final steps:** FO, cut the yarn leaving a 15 cm (6 in) tail for weaving in later. Weave in all ends using the tapestry needle.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Vanity Tray
- Stay organized – keep your yarn and materials close and tidy.
- Check your tension – make sure your stitches are even for a uniform look.
- Measure as you go – gauge and final sizes matter for the best outcome.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My tray is too small." – Check your stitch counts; you might have missed an increase round.
- "The stitches are uneven." – This can happen with varying tension; work on maintaining consistent pressure.
- "I can't fit my hook." – If your stitches are too tight, try using a larger hook to find a comfortable fit.

## How to Care for Your Vanity Tray
Hand wash your tray with mild soap and lay it flat to dry. Avoid harsh detergents to keep the fabric in good condition.

### FAQ
### Can I customize the size?
Yes! Adjust the number of increase rounds to make the tray larger or smaller as desired.

### What type of yarn should I use?
Using bulky cotton cord yarn gives the best structure and style. You can try other colors or textures if you like.

### How long will it take to finish?
Expect to spend about 2.5 hours in total: 20 minutes for the base, 2 hours for the sides, and 30 minutes for finishing.
