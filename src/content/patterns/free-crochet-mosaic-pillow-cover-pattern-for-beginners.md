This crochet mosaic pillow cover pattern features a stunning textured design in rich earth tones. It's a delightful project that adds a cozy touch to your home decor. You'll be working primarily with double crochet and half double crochet, making it both fun and straightforward to create.

This pattern is great for you if you're looking to enhance your crochet skills and create a beautiful, functional piece for your living space.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! While it's marked as intermediate, the basic stitches used are accessible to beginners. You'll have guided steps to help you through the mosaic technique, which is a wonderful way to build your skills.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- dc – double crochet
- hdc – half double crochet
- st – stitch
- sk – skip
- sp – space
- mosaic stitch – worked over the previous row's stitches; see special instructions for creating the mosaic effect.

## Size and Gauge
- Finished dimensions of the pillow cover: 46 × 46 cm (18 × 18 in).
- Gauge: 14 stitches and 10 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in the mosaic pattern. Ensure your gauge matches for the best fit.

## Step 1: Square Body
The pillow cover is worked in rows. 

1. **Foundation Chain:** 
   - ch 46.  
2. **Row 1:** 
   - dc in 4th ch from hook, dc in each ch across. Turn. (44 sts)  
3. **Row 2:** 
   - ch 3 (this counts as your first dc), *hdc in next st, dc in next st; repeat from * across to last st, hdc in last st. Turn. (44 sts)  
4. **Row 3:** 
   - Change to Color B, ch 3, *sk 1 st, dc in next st; repeat from * across. Turn. (22 sts)  
5. **Row 4:** 
   - ch 3, *hdc in first dc from previous row, dc in next skipped st; repeat from * across. Change to Color C at the end of the row. Turn. (44 sts)  
6. **Row 5:** 
   - ch 3, *dc in first st, hdc in next st; repeat from * across. Turn. (44 sts)  
7. **Row 6:** 
   - Change to Color D, ch 3, *sk 1 st, dc in next st; repeat from * across. Turn. (22 sts)  
8. **Row 7:** 
   - ch 3, *hdc in first dc from previous row, dc in next skipped st; repeat from * across using Color A. Turn. (44 sts)  
9. **Row 8:** 
   - Repeat Row 5. (44 sts)  
10. Repeat Rows 3–8 until you have a total of 15 rows in the mosaic pattern, finishing with Row 8.

## How to Join Your Pieces
1. Do not fasten off. Create a border:
   - Round 1: Ch 1, sc evenly around the entire edge of the pillow cover, making 3 sc in each corner. Join with a slip stitch to the first sc.
   - Round 2: Ch 1, sc in each sc around, join with slip stitch to the first sc.
   - Fasten off, and weave in the ends.
2. Place your pillow insert inside the cover.
3. Use the tapestry needle to weave through the stitches on the opening edge if needed to gather and close.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Pillow Cover
- Keep your workspace organized – having all your materials in one place helps maintain focus.
- Check your gauge before starting – it ensures your pillow cover will be the right size.
- Take breaks to avoid tension – relaxed hands create softer stitches.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My pillow cover is too small." – This could be due to an incorrect gauge. Always make a gauge swatch first.
- "The mosaic pattern doesn't look right." – Ensure you're following the stitch counts and changing colors correctly.
- "My edges curl up." – This can happen if your tension is too tight; try to loosen your grip slightly.

## How to Care for Your Pillow Cover
Hand wash or use a gentle cycle with cold water to avoid damaging the mosaic stitches. Lay flat to dry to help maintain the shape and texture of your pillow cover.
