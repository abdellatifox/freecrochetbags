## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern uses basic stitches that are easy to learn. You'll also have the chance to practice increasing stitches and working in rounds. Don't worry if you’re new to crochet; we'll guide you through each step.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- hdc – half double crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- st(s) – stitch(es)

## Size and Gauge
- Finished Size: 30 cm (12 in) diameter, 25 cm (10 in) height.
- Gauge: 12 sc and 14 rows = 10 cm (4 in) square.

To ensure your basket is the right size, check your gauge. If your stitches are too tight or too loose, adjust your hook size accordingly.

## Step 1: Making the Base
The base is worked in rounds.
1. Magic ring, ch 2 (does not count as a stitch).
2. Round 1: 10 sc into the magic ring, pull tight. (10 sts)
3. Round 2: *2 sc in each st* around. (20 sts)
4. Round 3: *1 sc, 2 sc in next st* around. (30 sts)
5. Round 4: *2 sc, 2 sc in next st* around. (40 sts)
6. Round 5: *3 sc, 2 sc in next st* around. (50 sts)
7. Round 6: *4 sc, 2 sc in next st* around. (60 sts)
8. Rounds 7–10: sc in each st around. (60 sts)

## Step 2: Crochet the Sides
The sides are worked in rounds.
9. Round 11: Change to Dusty Rose, sc in each st around. (60 sts)
10. Rounds 12–22: Change back to Natural Beige, sc in each st around. (60 sts)

## Step 3: Making the Handles (Make 2)
The handles are worked back and forth in rows.
1. Ch 25 with Natural Beige.
2. Row 1: Sc in 2nd ch from hook, sc in each ch to the end. (24 sts)
3. Rows 2–5: Ch 1, turn, sc across. (24 sts)
4. Finish off and leave a long tail for sewing.

## How to Assemble the Basket
1. Determine positions for handles; center them on the sides of the basket and use the yarn tails to sew securely in place.
2. Stuff the base lightly with stuffing if desired for added structure.
3. Weave in all ends using a tapestry needle.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Toy Storage Basket
- Keep your work organized with stitch markers to track your rounds.
- Maintain even tension throughout your stitches for a uniform look.
- Check the dimensions of your basket as you go to ensure the correct size.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My basket is too small. – You may not have worked enough increases in the base. Double-check the stitch counts and make sure to follow the instructions carefully.
- The stitches are uneven. – This often happens with tension issues. Try to keep your yarn relaxed as you crochet, and practice can help improve consistency.
- The basket slumps over. – This can occur if there aren’t enough stitches in the base or the sides. Make sure you’re following the pattern and using the correct number of stitches.

## How to Care for Your Toy Storage Basket
To keep your basket looking its best, gently hand wash it and lay it flat to dry. Avoid using bleach or harsh chemicals to maintain the integrity of your yarn.
