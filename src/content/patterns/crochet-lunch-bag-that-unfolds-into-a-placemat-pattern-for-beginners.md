This crochet lunch bag pattern makes a practical and stylish companion for your meals. The Lunch Unroll is designed to unfold into a spacious placemat, perfect for lunchtime at the office or a picnic. It uses basic stitches like single crochet and double crochet, making it easy and enjoyable for you to create.

This pattern is great for beginners who want to learn to crochet a functional item. You'll pick up helpful techniques for shaping and finishing as you work through the bag.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! With basic stitches and straightforward instructions, you'll feel confident as you crochet your very own lunch bag.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- sl st – slip stitch

## Size and Gauge
- Finished Bag Dimensions: 30.5 × 20.3 × 10.2 cm (12 × 8 × 4 in)
- Placemat dimensions (when unfolded): 30.5 × 40.6 cm (12 × 16 in)
- Gauge: 12 dc and 8 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in sc.

Check your gauge by making a small swatch. If needed, adjust your hook size for a tighter or looser gauge.

## Step 1: Body of the Bag
The body is worked in rows.

1. **Foundation Row:** With Grove Green, ch 46.
2. **Row 1:** Sc in 2nd ch from the hook and in each ch across. (45 sc)
3. **Row 2:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in each sc across. (45 sc)
4. **Row 3:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in each sc across. (45 sc)
5. **Row 4-12:** Repeat Row 2 for 10 rows total. (45 sc)
6. **Row 13 (Bottom Edge):** Ch 1, turn. Work 5 sc in the first st, then sc across until 5 sts remain. Work 5 sc in the last st. (55 sc)
7. **Row 14:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in each st across. (55 sc)
8. **Row 15-16:** Repeat Row 14 for 2 more rows. (55 sc)
9. **Row 17:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in the first 5 sc, then work 5 sc in the next st, *sk 1 st, sc in next st* repeat until the last 5 sc. (45 sc)
10. **Row 18-19:** Repeat Row 14 for 2 more rows. (45 sc)

## Step 2: Sides of the Bag
The sides continue with additional rows.

11. **Row 20:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in each st across. (45 sc)
12. **Row 21:** Ch 1, turn. Work 2 sc in the first sc, then sc in each st across. (46 sc)
13. **Row 22:** Ch 1, turn. Work 2 sc in the first sc, then sc in each st across. (47 sc)
14. **Row 23-30:** Repeat Row 20 for 8 more rows. (47 sc)

## Step 3: Top Border
We finish off the top with a border for a polished look.

15. **Row 31:** Ch 1, turn. Work a sc in the first 23 sts, ch 8, sk next 1 st, sc in next 23 sts. (47 sc, 8 ch)
16. **Row 32:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in first 23 sc, 8 sc in ch, sc in next 23 sc. (47 sc)
17. **Row 33:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in each st across. (47 sc)

## Finishing the Bag
To shape and finish your bag:

1. Fold the bag in half with the right sides together and join with sl st down both sides. 
2. Turn the bag right side out and block if necessary.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Bag
- Keep your yarn untangled – use a yarn bowl or bag while working.
- Maintain consistent tension – try to keep your stitches even.
- Check your size as you go – periodically measure to ensure the final product meets your expectations.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My bag is too small." – This might happen if your tension is too tight. Keep a looser grip on your yarn while crocheting.
- "The stitches are uneven." – This can happen with inconsistent tension. Use stitch markers to help keep your stitches organized.
- "I can't get my edge straight." – If the top isn't straight, re-check your counting to ensure you followed the same number of stitches in each row.

## How to Care for Your Lunch Bag
To keep your bag looking its best, hand wash gently and lay flat to dry. This will help maintain its shape and durability.

### FAQ
### Can I customize the size?
Yes! You can adjust the number of foundation chains to make the bag larger or smaller, but be sure to maintain the gauge for a consistent look.

### What if I want to add a lining?
Adding a fabric lining can give your bag extra sturdiness. Cut the fabric to match the dimensions of the bag and sew it in after finishing.
