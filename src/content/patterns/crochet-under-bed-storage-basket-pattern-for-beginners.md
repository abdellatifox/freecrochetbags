This crochet under-bed storage basket pattern makes a spacious and stylish solution for organizing items under your bed. With a pull strap for easy access, this basket is functional and enjoyable to make. You'll be using basic stitches, including single crochet and slip stitches, which makes this project great fun for intermediate crocheters.

This pattern is perfect for anyone looking to declutter their space while learning to crochet larger projects. You'll gain experience with shaping and working in continuous rounds, which are both essential skills in your crochet journey.

## Why You'll Love This Under-Bed Storage Basket
- Spacious design – holds plenty of items, ideal for organizing toys, linens, or storage.
- Convenient pull strap – easily access your items from under your bed without strain.
- Stylish addition – enhances your room's decor while keeping things neat and tidy.
- Quick project – completes in approximately 6 hours, making it a satisfying make.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! While this pattern is categorized as intermediate, you’ll grasp essential techniques that are great for expanding your skills. The use of basic stitches and the step-by-step instructions will help you feel confident as you create.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- inc – increase (2 sc in the same stitch)
- dec – decrease (sc two stitches together)

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished Size:** Approximately 71 × 36 × 25 cm (28 × 14 × 10 in)
- **Gauge:** 8 sc and 8 rows = 10 cm (4 in)

Make sure to check your gauge before starting. This will help ensure that your basket turns out the right size.

## Step 1: Making the Basket Body
The basket body is worked in rounds.  
1. **Round 1:** Ch 4, make a ring with sl st in first ch. (1 st)  
2. **Round 2:** Ch 1, 6 sc into the ring. (6 sc)  
3. **Round 3:** Ch 1, (inc) 6 times around. (12 sc)  
4. **Round 4:** Ch 1, (1 sc, inc) 6 times around. (18 sc)  
5. **Round 5:** Ch 1, (2 sc, inc) 6 times around. (24 sc)  
6. **Round 6:** Ch 1, (3 sc, inc) 6 times around. (30 sc)  
7. **Round 7:** Ch 1, (4 sc, inc) 6 times around. (36 sc)  
8. **Round 8:** Ch 1, (5 sc, inc) 6 times around. (42 sc)  
9. **Rounds 9–25:** Ch 1, sc in each st around. (42 sc)  
10. **Round 26:** Ch 1, (5 sc, dec) 6 times around. (36 sc)  
11. **Round 27:** Ch 1, (4 sc, dec) 6 times around. (30 sc)  
12. **Round 28:** Ch 1, (3 sc, dec) 6 times around. (24 sc)  

## Step 2: Pull Strap
The pull strap is worked in rows.  
13. **Row 29:** Ch 1, (2 sc, dec) 6 times around. (18 sc)  
14. **Row 30:** Ch 1, (1 sc, dec) 6 times around. (12 sc)  
15. **Row 31:** Ch 1, dec around. (6 sc)  
16. Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing.

## Step 3: Assembly
- Fold the top edge of the basket to create a finished border. Slip stitch the cut edge neatly along the top.  
- Attach the pull strap to the center of one longer side of the basket using the long tail from your last round. Sew securely.  
- Optional: Sew a button onto the strap for embellishment.  

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Basket
- Use stitch markers to track your rounds and avoid losing count.
- Remember to keep your tension even throughout to maintain a consistent shape.
- Regularly check your gauge to ensure your basket will fit as expected.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My basket is too small." – Check your gauge; if it's off, adjust your hook size.
- "The stitches are loose." – Ensure your tension is consistent as you crochet your rounds.
- "There are gaps in my basket." – Pull tight at the joining stitches to avoid holes between rounds.

## How to Care for Your Basket
Hand wash in cool water and lay flat to dry. Avoid ironing, as this can distort the shape of your basket.
