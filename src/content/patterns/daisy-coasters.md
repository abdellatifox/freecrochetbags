These **crochet daisy coasters** are one of the quickest and happiest projects you can make. Each one takes about 30 minutes, uses tiny amounts of yarn, and brightens up any table. A set of four makes a lovely handmade gift.

This **easy crochet pattern for beginners** also teaches you the **magic ring**, a skill you'll use in lots of other projects.

## Why You'll Love These Daisy Coasters

- **Super quick** – finish one in a single sitting.
- **Scrap-friendly** – perfect for leftover cotton.
- **Learn new skills** – magic ring and working in rounds.
- **Useful and pretty** – they protect your table in style.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)

- **ch** – chain
- **sl st** – slip stitch
- **sc** – single crochet
- **hdc** – half double crochet
- **dc** – double crochet
- **tr** – treble crochet: yarn over twice, insert hook, pull up a loop, then (yarn over, pull through 2 loops) three times.
- **magic ring** – an adjustable loop that closes tightly so there's no hole in the middle.

## How to Make a Magic Ring

1. Wrap the yarn around two fingers in a loop, with the tail hanging down.
2. Insert your hook under the loop, grab the working yarn and pull it through.
3. Ch 1 to lock it.
4. Work your stitches over the loop and the tail together.
5. Pull the tail to close the center tightly.

## Size

Each finished coaster measures about **10 cm (4 in)** across.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### The Yellow Center

With yellow, make a magic ring.

**Round 1:** Ch 1, work 8 sc into the ring. Pull the tail to close. Join with a sl st to the first sc. **(8 sc)**

**Round 2:** Ch 1, 2 sc in each stitch around. Join. **(16 sc)**

**Round 3:** Ch 1, *sc in the next stitch, 2 sc in the next stitch.* Repeat from * around. Join and fasten off. **(24 sc)**

### The Cream Petals

Join cream in any stitch with a sl st.

**Round 4:** *Ch 7. Working back down the chain, starting in the 2nd ch from the hook: sl st, sc, hdc, dc, dc, tr (one stitch in each chain). Skip 1 stitch on the center, sl st in the next stitch.* Repeat from * 11 more times. **(12 petals)**

> **What's happening?** Each petal starts as a chain. By working taller and taller stitches back along it, the petal gets wider toward the tip – just like a real daisy.

Fasten off and weave in the ends.

## Finishing Tip: Make Them Lie Flat

Place your coaster under a damp cloth and press gently with a warm iron, or pin it flat, spray with water and let it dry.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)

- **My center is wavy.** You may have too many stitches. Recount each round.
- **My center is cupping.** Crochet a little looser, or try a hook 0.5 mm bigger.
- **I have too many or too few petals.** Make sure you skip exactly one stitch between petals.

## FAQ

### What yarn is best for crochet coasters?

100% cotton is best because it absorbs moisture and can handle hot mugs. Acrylic can melt with very hot drinks.

### Can I wash crochet coasters?

Yes. Wash cotton coasters by hand or on a gentle cycle, then lay them flat to dry.

### How can I make the coasters bigger?

Add one more round to the center (*sc 2, 2 sc in the next* for 32 stitches) and make 16 petals.
