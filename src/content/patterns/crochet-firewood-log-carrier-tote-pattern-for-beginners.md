This crochet firewood log carrier tote pattern makes The Hearth Hauler, a practical and spacious bag for carrying firewood from outside to your cozy hearth. It features a reinforced bottom and sturdy handles, making it both easy to crochet and functional to use. We’ll be working primarily with double crochet and single crochet stitches, perfect for beginners ready to take on a small project.

This pattern is ideal for anyone looking to create a useful item for the home. You’ll learn how to construct a tote that balances durability with style.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern uses basic stitches, making it accessible for novice crocheters. The clear step-by-step instructions will guide you through each part, ensuring it's an enjoyable experience.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- sp – space

## Size and Gauge
- Finished dimensions: 46 × 30.5 cm (18 × 12 in) 
- Handle length: 61 cm (24 in)
- Gauge: 11 dc and 6 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in double crochet. Make sure to check your gauge by crocheting a swatch of 15 dc for 10 rows. Adjust your hook size if needed.

## Step 1: Base of the Tote
The base of the tote is worked in rows.
1. **Ch 28** (27 sts + 1 for turning).
2. **Row 1:** Dc in the 4th ch from the hook and in each ch across. (26 dc)
3. **Row 2:** Ch 3 (counts as first dc), turn, dc in each dc across. (26 dc)
4. **Rows 3-10:** Repeat Row 2 for a total of 9 rows. (26 dc)

## Step 2: Sides of the Tote
Now we’ll work the sides of the tote.
5. **Row 11:** Ch 1, turn, sc in first dc, *2 dc in next dc, 1 dc in each of the next 2 dc* repeat from * to * 8 times, 2 dc in last dc. (40 dc)
6. **Row 12:** Ch 3, turn, dc in each stitch across. (40 dc)
7. **Row 13:** Ch 3, turn, dc in first 2 dc, *2 dc in next dc, dc in next 3 dc* repeat from * to * 8 times, dc in last 2 dc. (52 dc)
8. **Rows 14-17:** Repeat Row 12 for a total of 4 rows. (52 dc)

## Step 3: Closing the Tote
We’ll start to finish off the body of the tote.
9. **Row 18:** Ch 1, turn, sc in first dc, *dc in each of the next 4 dc, sc in next* repeat from * to * until last 4 dc, dc in each of the last 4. (40 dc, 12 sc)
10. **Row 19:** Ch 3, turn, dc in each stitch across. (52 dc)

## Step 4: Handles
Next, let's create the handles.
11. **Row 20:** Ch 7, skip first ch, sc in remaining 6 ch. (6 sc)
12. **Row 21-22:** Ch 2 (counts as first dc), turn, dc in each sc. (6 dc)
13. **Row 23:** Ch 1, turn, sc in first dc, *dc in each of the next 3 dc, sc in next* repeat from * to *. (8 dc, 1 sc)
14. **Row 24-25:** Repeat Row 12. (8 dc)
15. **Row 26:** Ch 1, sc across. (8 sc)

## Finishing
16. Repeat Rows 20-26 for the second handle.
17. Attach handles to either side of the tote, 15 cm (6 in) from the top edge, with sc.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Tote
- Keep your workspace organized to avoid losing materials.
- Maintain consistent tension in your stitches for a uniform look.
- Check your size often as you work to ensure proper dimensions.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My tote is too small." – You might have skipped stitches. Count your stitches at the end of each row.
- "The stitches are uneven." – This often happens with varying tension. Practice keeping a steady tension when working.
- "The base feels floppy." – If you didn’t add stiff interfacing or cardboard, it may lack support. Consider inserting one for better strength.

## How to Care for Your Tote
To keep your tote looking great, spot clean or hand wash with mild soap, and lay it flat to dry. Avoid wringing the tote, as this can distort its shape.

## FAQ
### Can I customize the size?
Yes! To create a smaller tote, reduce the foundation chain by multiples of 2. Just be sure to adjust the sides accordingly.

### What yarn can I use instead?
You can substitute any bulky weight yarn, but try to use a cotton blend for durability. Check the gauge to ensure your final product maintains the right size.

### How long will this take to make?
Approximately 4.5 hours depending on your crocheting speed and experience level.
