With simple stitches like double crochet and single crochet, this project is enjoyable and beginner-friendly.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- inc – increase (2 stitches in same st)
- sk – skip

## Size and Gauge
- Finished Size: 30.5 × 20.3 cm (12 × 8 in) deep
- Gauge: 12 dc and 7 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in main stitch pattern. 

To check gauge, crochet a swatch of 20 dc x 14 rows. Adjust hook size accordingly to match gauge.

## Step 1: Body of the Caddy
The body is worked in rows.  
1. **Foundation Row:** Ch 40 (40 sts)  
2. **Row 1:** Dc in 4th ch from hook, dc in each ch across (38 dc)  
3. **Row 2:** Ch 3 (counts as dc), turn, dc in each st across (38 dc)  
4. **Rows 3–10:** Repeat Row 2 (38 dc each row)

## Step 2: Side Panel
5. **Row 11:** Ch 1, sc in first st, *inc in next st, sc in next 3 sts*; repeat from * to * until the end of the row with an inc in last st (47 sts)  
6. **Rows 12–14:** Ch 1, turn, sc in each st across (47 sc)  
7. **Row 15:** Ch 1, sc in first 20 sts, ch 30 (for strap), skip the next 7 sts, sc in last 20 sts (47 sts)

## Step 3: Strap
8. **Row 16:** Ch 1, sc in first 20 sts, sc in each ch (30 sc), sc in last 20 sts (82 sts)  
9. **Row 17:** Ch 1, turn, sc in each st across (82 sc)

## Step 4: Finish Off
10. **Row 18:** Ch 1, turn, sc in first 5 sts, ch 1, skip 6 sts, sc in next 5 sts, ch 1, skip 6 sts, sc in last 5 sts (82 sts)

Finish off and weave in all ends.

## Assembly & Finishing
Fold the caddy in half vertically, then whip stitch the sides together to form a pocket using the same yarn for a seamless look. If you’re using cardboard for support, cut it to size and insert it into the bottom before fully joining the sides.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Bedside Caddy
- Stay organized by keeping all materials within reach.
- Maintain consistent tension to ensure even stitches throughout your project.
- Regularly check the size to avoid any discrepancies.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My caddy is too small." – This may happen if you didn’t chain enough for the foundation. Simply add more chains next time.
- "The stitches are uneven." – This can occur from varying tension. Try to keep a steady tension as you crochet.
- "I lost count of my stitches." – Count your stitches at the end of each row. If you miss any, it's easy to frog (undo) a few stitches and fix it.

## How to Care for Your Bedside Caddy
To keep your caddy looking fresh, hand wash it in cold water and lay it flat to dry. This will help maintain its shape and extend the life of your crochet project.
