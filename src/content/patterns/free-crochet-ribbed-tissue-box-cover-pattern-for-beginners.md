This free crochet ribbed tissue box cover pattern makes a stylish and cozy addition to any room. It's designed specifically for beginners and introduces you to basic crochet techniques while creating a functional home accessory. You’ll learn how to work with stitches like single crochet and half double crochet, making it enjoyable and satisfying.

The finished tissue box cover fits standard tissue boxes and brings a pop of color to your decor. Let’s get started!

- Stylish design – adds a decorative touch to your home.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern uses basic stitches and clear instructions, making it perfect for beginners. You’ll gain confidence in your crocheting skills as you create a useful item.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- hdc – half double crochet
- st – stitch
- yo – yarn over

## Size and Gauge
- **Gauge:** 12 sc and 14 rows = 10 cm (4 in)
- **Finished Size:** 14 × 14 × 15.2 cm (5.5 × 5.5 × 6 in)

## Step 1: Body of the Cover
The body of the tissue box cover is worked in rows.

1. **Foundation Round:**  
   Ch 26.

2. **Row 1:**  
   Starting in the second chain from the hook, sc across (25 sc).

3. **Row 2:**  
   Ch 1, turn.  
   hdc in the first st and in each st across (25 hdc).

4. **Row 3:**  
   Ch 1, turn.  
   sc in the first st and in each st across (25 sc).

5. **Row 4:**  
   Ch 1, turn.  
   hdc in the first st and in each st across (25 hdc).

6. **Repeat Rows 3 and 4:**  
   Continue alternating sc and hdc for a total of 20 rows.

7. **Final Row:**  
   After 20 rows, ch 1, turn.  
   sc across (25 sc).

8. **Shape the Cover:**  
   Fold the piece to form a box shape by bringing the short edges together, aligning the stitches.  
   Join the edges using a slip stitch or whip stitch through the loops (25 stitches joined).

## Finishing Your Tissue Box Cover
1. Weave in all ends using the tapestry needle.
2. Ensure the cover fits snugly over your tissue box and adjust if necessary.
3. Optionally, you may add decorative elements like a contrasting trim around the box's opening by crocheting a round of sc in Color B.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Tissue Box Cover
- Keep your yarn organized to avoid tangles.
- Check your tension regularly to ensure even stitches.
- Measure your cover against the tissue box frequently to maintain the right fit.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My box cover is too small. – You may not have added enough foundation chains. Count again and ensure you’re working in multiples of two for larger boxes.
- The stitches are uneven. – This often happens from inconsistent tension. Focus on keeping your yarn tension steady for uniform stitches.
- I lost my place while crocheting. – Use stitch markers to help keep track of your rows and avoid confusion.

## How to Care for Your Tissue Box Cover
Hand wash your cover in cold water and lay it flat to dry. Avoid bleach to keep your colors vibrant.

### FAQ
### Can I customize the size? 
Yes! To accommodate larger tissue boxes, add extra chains in multiples of 2 to your foundation chain. This gives you a snug fit for different box sizes.

### What type of yarn can I use instead? 
You can substitute with any worsted weight cotton yarn in your favorite colors. Just ensure it matches gauge for the best results.
