This crochet detachable pouch pattern makes a stylish and functional accessory that you can clip onto any bag strap. It’s perfect for carrying small essentials like keys, coins, or cards. With a modern look and simple stitches, this project is both enjoyable and great for beginners.

You'll learn how to create a textured pouch and flap, attach a zipper, and add a lobster clasp. It's an excellent way to practice your double crochet and finishing techniques!

## Why You'll Love This Detachable Pouch
- Stylish and functional – perfect for keeping your small items organized and easily accessible.
- Quick to make – you can finish this pouch in about 3 hours.
- Customizable – change the colors for a personal touch or use variegated yarn for added flair.
- Great for beginners – simple stitches and straightforward assembly make it super doable!

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This project uses basic double crochet stitches and simple assembly techniques. You'll learn new skills while making something useful.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- dc – double crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- sp – space
- st – stitch
- sc – single crochet

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished size:** Approximately 12.7 cm × 10.2 cm (5 in × 4 in)
- **Gauge:** 12 dc and 8 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in double crochet.

To check your gauge, make a swatch of 20 dc in a square, then measure. Adjust your hook size as needed to match the gauge.

## Step 1: Making the Pouch Body
The pouch body is worked in rows. Start with the foundation chain.
1. Ch 22.
2. **Row 1:** Dc in 4th ch from hook, dc in each ch across (20 dc).
3. **Row 2:** Ch 2, turn, dc in each st across (20 dc).
4. **Row 3:** Ch 2, turn, dc in the back loop only of each st across (20 dc).
5. **Row 4–10:** Repeat Row 2 (20 dc each).
6. **Row 11:** Ch 2, turn, *dc in first, dc2tog* across to last st, dc in last (11 dc).
7. **Row 12:** Ch 2, turn, dc in each st across (11 dc).
8. **Row 13:** Ch 2, turn, dc in the back loop only of each st across (11 dc).
9. **Row 14–18:** Repeat Row 12 (11 dc each).
10. **Row 19:** Ch 2, turn, *dc in first, dc2tog* across to last st, dc in last (6 dc).
11. **Row 20:** Ch 1, turn, sc across (6 sc).
12. Fasten off, leaving a long tail for seaming.

## Step 2: Making the Pouch Flap
The flap is worked separately and then sewn onto the main body.
1. **Flap Foundation:** Ch 10.
2. **Row 1:** Dc in 4th ch from hook, dc in each ch across (8 dc).
3. **Row 2–5:** Ch 2, turn, dc in each st across (8 dc).
4. **Row 6:** Ch 2, turn, *dc in first, dc2tog* across to last st, dc in last (5 dc).
5. **Row 7:** Ch 2, turn, dc in each st across (5 dc).
6. **Row 8:** Ch 2, turn, dc in the back loop only of each st across (5 dc).
7. **Row 9:** Ch 2, turn, *dc in first, dc2tog* across to last st, dc in last (3 dc).
8. **Row 10:** Ch 1, turn, sc across (3 sc).
9. Fasten off, leaving a long tail for seaming.

## Step 3: Assembly & Finishing
1. **Seaming:** Align the top of the pouch with the flap. Using the long tail from the main body, sew the flap in place securely with a whip stitch on the back.
2. **Adding the Zipper:** Pin the zipper onto the top opening of the pouch. Using a tapestry needle and the same yarn, slip stitch along the zipper edge to attach it to the pouch.
3. **Attach Lobster Clasp:** Use a small piece of leftover yarn to attach the lobster clasp to the side of the pouch, creating a loop big enough for the clasp to fit through.
4. **Weaving in Ends:** Weave in all remaining ends using a tapestry needle.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Detachable Pouch
- Stay organized – keep your materials and tools close at hand.
- Check your tension regularly to ensure consistent sizing.
- Measure your work as you go to make sure it matches the finished dimensions.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My pouch is too small." – You may have miscounted your stitches. Double-check that you're following the pattern accurately.
- "The stitches are uneven." – This often happens with inconsistent tension. Practice keeping an even grip while crocheting.
- "The zipper isn't aligned." – Make sure to pin the zipper well before stitching so it stays in place.

## How to Care for Your Detachable Pouch
Hand wash in cold water and lay flat to dry. Avoid bleach and ironing to keep your pouch looking its best.

### FAQ
### Can I customize the size?
Yes! You can adjust the foundation chain to make a larger or smaller pouch. Just remember to keep the proportions the same for the main body and flap.

### What if I don’t have a lobster clasp?
You can use any clip or keychain ring to attach the pouch to your bag strap. Get creative!

### How long will it take to make?
This project takes about 3 hours in total. It can be done in smaller sessions if you like.
