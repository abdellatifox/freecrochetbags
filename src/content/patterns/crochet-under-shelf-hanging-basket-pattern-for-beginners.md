This crochet under-shelf hanging basket pattern makes a delightful and functional "Shelf Sling" for your home. It's perfect for holding fruits, herbs, or other small items while allowing for airflow and adding a decorative touch. The open mesh pattern is both enjoyable to make and practical for everyday use.

This project is suitable for beginners and more experienced crocheters alike. You’ll learn how to create an adjustable length and explore basic stitches like double crochet and single crochet.

## Why You'll Love This Shelf Sling
- Practical and decorative – a great way to organize your space while showcasing your items.
- Easy to customize – use different colors or adjust the size for a personalized look.
- Quick to make – complete this project in just a few hours, making it ideal for last-minute gifts.
- Fun finishing touches – include beads or stiffening for extra charm and support.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- DC – double crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- st/sts – stitch/stitches
- sp – space
- SC – single crochet

## Gauge and Finished Size
- Finished Size: Approximately 25 cm × 13 cm (10 in × 5 in) deep with an adjustable strap length
- Gauge: 12 DC and 8 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in double crochet

## How to Crochet the Shelf Sling (Step by Step)

### Body of the Basket
The body of the basket is worked in rows.  

1. **Foundation Row:** Ch 36.  
2. **Row 1:** DC in the 4th ch from the hook and in each ch across. Turn. (34 DC)  
3. **Row 2–10:** Ch 3 (counts as your first DC), DC in each st across. Turn. (34 DC)  
   *Repeat for a total of 9 more rows. You should have 10 rows of DC.*

4. **Row 11:** Ch 3, *DC in the next 17 sts, ch 1, skip 1 st,* repeat from * to *, DC in last 17 sts. Turn. (34 DC + 1 ch)  
5. **Row 12–20:** Ch 3, DC in each st and ch 1 space across. Turn. (34 DC + 1 ch)  
   *Repeat for 8 more rows, finishing with 20 rows total.*

6. **Row 21:** Ch 1, *SC in the next 17 sts, skip 1 st,* repeat from * to *, SC in last 16 sts. Turn. (34 SC)

7. **Row 22:** Ch 3, DC in each st across. Turn. (34 DC)  
   *This row will help in creating a neat border for the edge of the basket.*

8. **Finishing the Body:** Fasten off and weave in all ends.

### Making the Hanging Mechanism
The straps are worked separately.  

1. **Straps:**  
   - Ch 80.  
   - Row 1: SC in each ch across. Turn. (80 SC)  
   - Row 2: Ch 1, turn. SC in each SC. Fasten off. (80 SC)  
   - Make 2 straps.

2. **Attaching Straps to Basket:**  
   - Position the straps evenly across the top of the basket, pinning them in place.  
   - Use the tapestry needle to sew the straps firmly to the basket edge with matching yarn.

### Adding Wooden Dowel
1. Use the tapestry needle to thread the yarn through the wooden dowel.  
2. Tie a knot to secure it on each end. This will add structural support to the basket.

### Attach Beads (Optional)
1. Thread the wooden beads onto the ends of the straps for a decorative touch.

## Finishing Your Shelf Sling
- Ensure all ends are woven in securely.
- If stiffening is desired, use stiffening spray as directed and allow to dry fully.
- Carefully block the basket for a neat finish and even shape.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Shelf Sling
- Stay organized by counting your stitches to avoid mistakes.
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rows, if needed.
- Regularly check your gauge to ensure your basket has the right size.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My stitches are too tight." – This is likely due to holding the yarn too tightly. Relax your hands and try again.
- "The basket shape is uneven." – Ensure you're following the stitch counts and rows correctly. Count your stitches each row.
- "My basket is too big." – Check your gauge. You may need a smaller hook to match the required gauge.

## How to Care for Your Shelf Sling
To keep your basket looking its best, hand wash it and lay it flat to dry. Avoid machine washing to preserve its shape and integrity.

### FAQ
### Can I customize the size?
Yes! You can adjust the chain length in the foundation row for a wider basket or modify the row counts for a deeper one.

### What type of yarn can I use?
Worsted weight cotton works best for this pattern. You can also use a cotton-blend for a bit more durability.

### How long will it take to finish?
The total time to complete the Shelf Sling is approximately 2 to 2.5 hours, depending on your skill level and experience.
