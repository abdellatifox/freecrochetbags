This crochet pouf cover pattern makes a cozy, functional accessory for any space. The Tray Pouf serves a dual purpose as a pouf for seating or a footrest while featuring a convenient flip-top tray for placing items. Using basic stitches like single crochet combined with a few increases and decreases, this project is enjoyable to crochet.

This pattern is perfect for intermediate crocheters looking to enhance their skills, particularly in constructing unique shapes. You'll learn how to make a structured pouf complete with a decorative tray on top, making it both practical and stylish.

## Why You'll Love This Tray Pouf
- Versatile design – use it as extra seating or as a stylish footrest.
- Built-in flip-top tray – provides a convenient surface for books or coffee cups.
- Customizable colors – mix and match shades to fit your decor style.
- Quick to make – a satisfying project that takes only about 8.5 hours to complete.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! While this pattern is marked as intermediate, beginners can tackle it with some patience. By following the clear step-by-step instructions and practicing basic stitches, you’ll be able to create this lovely pouf.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- inc – increase (2 sc in the same stitch)
- dec – decrease (single crochet two stitches together)

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished Size:** Approximately 46 cm diameter and 30 cm tall (18 × 12 in)
- **Gauge:** 8 sc and 9 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in single crochet.

Make sure to check your gauge by crocheting a small swatch. If your gauge doesn’t match, you may need to change your hook size.

## Step 1: Making the Pouf Base
The pouf base is worked in rounds. 
1. Start with a magic circle.
2. Round 1: Ch 1, work 6 sc in the circle. Join with a sl st to the first sc. (6 sts)
3. Round 2: Inc in each stitch around. Join. (12 sts)
4. Round 3: *1 sc, inc* repeat around. Join. (18 sts)
5. Round 4: *2 sc, inc* repeat around. Join. (24 sts)
6. Round 5: *3 sc, inc* repeat around. Join. (30 sts)
7. Round 6: *4 sc, inc* repeat around. Join. (36 sts)
8. Rounds 7–14: Sc in each stitch around for 8 rounds. (36 sts)
9. Round 15: *4 sc, dec* repeat around. Join. (30 sts)
10. Round 16: *3 sc, dec* repeat around. Join. (24 sts)
11. Round 17: *2 sc, dec* repeat around. Join. (18 sts)
12. Round 18: *1 sc, dec* repeat around. Join. (12 sts)
13. Stuff the pouf base.
14. Round 19: Dec around until closed. (6 sts). Fasten off.

## Step 2: Crochet the Side Walls
Attach yarn to any stitch of the top edge.
1. Round 1: Ch 1, sc in each stitch around. Join. (36 sts)
2. Rounds 2–12: Sc in each stitch for 11 rounds. Join. 
3. Fasten off.

## Step 3: Crochet the Tray Top
Using the 5 mm hook and Dark Brown yarn, start with a magic circle.
1. Round 1: Ch 1, work 10 sc in circle. Join. (10 sts)
2. Round 2: Inc in each stitch around. Join. (20 sts)
3. Round 3: *1 sc, inc* repeat around. Join. (30 sts)
4. Round 4: *2 sc, inc* repeat around. Join. (40 sts)
5. Rounds 5–6: Sc in each stitch for 2 rounds. Fasten off.

## Step 4: Crochet the Tray Sides
Attach Dark Brown yarn to any stitch of the outer edge of the tray top.
1. Round 1: Ch 1, work sc in each stitch. Join. (40 sts)
2. Rounds 2–8: Sc in each stitch for 7 rounds. Join. 
3. Fasten off.

## Assembly & Finishing
1. **Join the tray:** Sew the tray side to the tray top using the whip stitch.
2. **Secure the pouf top:** Use the tapestry needle to attach the tray to the top of the pouf cover by sewing through the rim, ensuring it can flip open.
3. **Add buttons:** Sew the buttons onto the underside of the tray to secure the tray when closed.
4. **Weave in all ends** and block the pouf to shape.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Tray Pouf
- Keep your workspace tidy – it makes it easier to find everything you need.
- Check your stitches after each round to maintain even tension.
- Measure the pouf as you go to ensure it matches the final dimensions.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My pouf is lopsided." – This can happen with uneven tension. Try to keep your stitches consistent.
- "The tray top doesn’t fit." – Ensure you counted stitches correctly in the rounds. If in doubt, check your counts as you go.
- "My pouf is too small." – If your gauge is tighter, you might need a larger hook to get the correct size.

## How to Care for Your Tray Pouf
To keep your pouf looking its best, gently hand wash it in cold water and lay it flat to dry. This will help maintain the shape and integrity of the yarn.
