This crochet basket pattern makes a sturdy and stylish chunky basket ideal for holding blankets or organizing home items. With its textured surface and wide opening, this project is both easy and enjoyable, making it perfect for beginners. You'll work primarily with single crochet stitches, which are simple to master.

Whether you're looking to spruce up your home or gift a unique basket, this pattern will guide you through every step with clear instructions and helpful tips.

## Why You'll Love This Chunky Basket
- Quick to crochet – you can complete it in about 4–5 hours!
- Stylish and practical – perfect for home organization and decor.
- Uses only basic stitches – you’ll be using single crochet throughout.
- Flexible design – easily adjust the size or mix and match colors for a personalized touch.

## Skill Level: Easy
This pattern is great for beginners! You’ll learn how to crochet in the round and work with a chunky yarn that makes the process even faster and more fun.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- Rnd – round

## Size and Gauge
- Finished size: 30.5 cm diameter × 25.5 cm high (12 in × 10 in)
- Gauge: 6 sc and 5 rows = 10 cm (4 in)

Measuring your gauge is important for ensuring your basket turns out the correct size. If your swatch differs, try changing your hook size.

## Step 1: Making the Base
The base is worked in rounds.  
- Ch 4, sl st to first ch to form a ring. (1 st)  
- Rnd 1: Ch 1, 8 sc in ring, sl st to first sc. (8 sts)  
- Rnd 2: Ch 1, 2 sc in each st around, sl st to first sc. (16 sts)  
- Rnd 3: Ch 1, *sc in next st, 2 sc in next st; repeat from * around, sl st to first sc. (24 sts)  
- Rnd 4: Ch 1, *sc in next 2 sts, 2 sc in next st; repeat from * around, sl st to first sc. (32 sts)  
- Rnd 5: Ch 1, *sc in next 3 sts, 2 sc in next st; repeat from * around, sl st to first sc. (40 sts)  
- Rnd 6: Ch 1, *sc in next 4 sts, 2 sc in next st; repeat from * around, sl st to first sc. (48 sts)  

## Step 2: Crochet the Body
The body is worked in rounds.  
- Rnd 7-16: Ch 1, sc in each st around, sl st to first sc. (48 sts)  
At the end of Round 16, the height of the basket should be approximately 25.5 cm (10 in).

## Step 3: Finishing
- Rnd 17: Ch 1, sc in each st around, sl st to first sc. (48 sts)  
- Fasten off and weave in all ends. 

## How to Attach Stiff Interfacing (Optional)
- Cut stiff interfacing to fit the inner sides of the basket.
- Use fabric glue or sew the interfacing in place for added firmness.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Basket
- Keep your stitches even – this will help your basket maintain its shape.
- Count your stitches regularly to avoid missing any.
- Use stitch markers to mark important rounds or stitches.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My basket is oddly shaped." – This often comes from uneven tension. Keep your stitches consistent.
- "The base is too small." – Check if you completed the correct number of increases in the base rounds.
- "My yarn splits." – Use a soft touch and consider a hook with a different material or finish to help.

## How to Care for Your Basket
For best results, hand wash your basket in cold water and lay it flat to dry. Avoid machine washing to keep its shape intact.

### FAQ
### Can I customize the size?
Yes! To make a larger basket, just increase the number of rounds in the body as desired.

### What yarn can I use as a substitute?
You can use any super bulky yarn, but make sure to check the gauge for a similar effect.

### How long will this project take?
This basket typically takes about 4-5 hours depending on your crochet speed and practice level.
