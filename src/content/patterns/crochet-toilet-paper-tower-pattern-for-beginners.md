This crochet toilet paper tower pattern makes a stylish and functional holder for your bathroom. The Roll Drop features a clever pull-out slot, so you can easily dispense your next roll. With simple stitches like single crochet and slip stitch, this project is enjoyable and achievable for intermediate crocheters.

You'll learn helpful techniques, such as increasing and decreasing, while creating an attractive piece that keeps your space organized. It's perfect for anyone looking to add a personal touch to their home decor.

## Why You'll Love This Toilet Paper Tower
- Space-saving design – Perfect for storing multiple rolls in a compact way.
- Convenient pull-out slot – Easily access your toilet paper without hassle.
- Stylish addition to your decor – Adds a personal and modern touch to your bathroom.
- Quick project – Complete it in about 6 hours with a little dedication.

## Skill Level: Intermediate
This pattern includes techniques like increasing and decreasing, which require some experience with crochet. If you've mastered single crochet and are ready for a fun challenge, you'll do great!

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain  
- sc – single crochet  
- sl st – slip stitch  
- st(s) – stitch(es)  
- inc – increase (2 sc in the same st)  
- dec – decrease (single crochet 2 stitches together)  

## Size and Gauge
- **Gauge:** 12 stitches and 14 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in single crochet.  
- **Finished size:** Approximately 40.6 cm (16 in) tall and 15.2 cm (6 in) in diameter.

## Step 1: Body of the Tower
The body of the tower is worked in rounds. 
1. **Foundation:**  
   Ch 4, join with sl st to form a ring. (1 st)
   
2. **Round 1:**  
   Ch 1, 6 sc in the ring, join with sl st to the first sc. (6 sts)

3. **Round 2:**  
   Ch 1, inc in each st around, join with sl st. (12 sts)

4. **Round 3:**  
   Ch 1, *sc in the next st, inc in the next st* repeat around, join. (18 sts)

5. **Round 4:**  
   Ch 1, *sc in the next 2 sts, inc in the next st* repeat around, join. (24 sts)

6. **Rounds 5–12 (8 rounds total):**  
   Ch 1, sc in each st around, join. (24 sts each round)

7. **Round 13:**  
   Ch 1, *sc in the next 3 sts, dec* repeat around, join. (18 sts)

8. **Round 14:**  
   Ch 1, *sc in the next 2 sts, dec* repeat around, join. (12 sts)

9. **Round 15:**  
   Ch 1, *sc in the next st, dec* repeat around, join. (6 sts)

10. **Finish:**  
   Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing.

## Step 2: Pull-Out Slot
The pull-out slot is worked in rows. 
1. **Foundation:**  
   With Soft White yarn, ch 14. (14 sts)

2. **Row 1:**  
   Sc in the 2nd ch from the hook and in each ch across. (13 sts)

3. **Rows 2–6:**  
   Ch 1, turn, sc in each st across. (13 sts) 

4. **Create a flap:**  
   Row 7: Ch 1, turn, sc in the next 7 sts, ch 1, turn.

5. **Row 8:**  
   Sc in the next 3 sts, sl st in the next 1 st, sc in the last 3 sts, cut yarn and fasten off.

## Step 3: Assembly & Finishing
1. **Assembly of the Body:**  
   Use the long tail from the body to sew the top edge of the body in a circular motion to form the rim of the tower.

2. **Attach the Pull-Out Slot:**  
   Position the pull-out slot on the chosen edge of the body tower. Sew securely using yarn. Ensure that the pull-out section opens easily.

3. **Button Closure:**  
   Sew a button on the edge of the pull-out slot for easier access and aesthetic appeal.

4. **Weave in any loose ends** using a tapestry needle.

5. **Optional:** If desired, you can stuff the body with fiberfill for additional support.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Toilet Paper Tower
- Keep your stitch tension consistent throughout the project.
- Use stitch markers to help keep track of your rounds.
- Check your gauge before starting to ensure the right fit and size.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My tower is too small." – Check if you missed any rounds or decreased too early. Count your stitches regularly.
- "The pull-out slot is too tight." – Make sure you followed the correct stitch counts for the slot. Adjust if necessary.
- "My stitches are uneven." – Focus on maintaining an even tension as you crochet. Practice a bit if needed.

## How to Care for Your Toilet Paper Tower
Hand wash in cold water and lay flat to dry to maintain its shape and structure. Avoid wringing out the yarn to prevent distortion.

### FAQ

Yes! You can make a taller or shorter tower by adjusting the number of rounds in the body.

### What yarn substitutions can I use?
You can use any worsted weight cotton yarn in your desired colors as long as you check your gauge for the correct fit.
