This crochet baby pom-pom hat pattern makes a cozy and adorable accessory for chilly winter days. With its soft texture and playful pom-poms, it’s perfect for keeping little heads warm and stylish. This pattern is beginner-friendly, featuring simple stitches that make it enjoyable, and it covers sizes for babies aged 0 to 12 months.

You'll learn how to create a beautiful hat while practicing essential crochet skills like the double crochet and single crochet. Plus, you can customize your hat with fun color combinations.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern uses basic stitches such as double crochet and single crochet, making it an excellent choice for those new to crochet. It guides you through each step, ensuring you feel confident as you create a lovely hat.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sl st – slip stitch
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- st – stitch
- sts – stitches
- FLO – front loop only

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished size:** Approximately 35.5 cm (14 in) in circumference and 15 cm (6 in) in height.
- **Size-specific measurements:**
  - Newborn size: Approx. 30.5 cm (12 in) circumference (same height).
  - 3-6 months: Approx. 35.5 cm (14 in) circumference (same height).
- **Gauge:** 14 dc and 10 rows = 10 cm (4 in). Make sure to swatch to ensure you get the correct size.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Hat Body
The hat body is worked in rounds to create a cozy fit.

1. **Foundation Round:** 
   - Ch 4, join with sl st to form a ring. (1 st)
2. **Round 1:** 
   - Ch 3 (counts as first dc), 11 dc in ring, join with sl st to top of ch 3. (12 sts)
3. **Round 2:** 
   - Ch 3, dc in same st, 2 dc in each st around, join with sl st. (24 sts)
4. **Round 3:** 
   - Ch 3, dc in same st, *dc in next st, 2 dc in next st*, repeat from * to * around, join with sl st. (36 sts)
5. **Round 4:** 
   - Ch 3, dc in same st, *dc in next 2 sts, 2 dc in next st*, repeat from * to * around, join with sl st. (48 sts)
6. **Rounds 5–10:** 
   - Ch 3, dc in each st around, join with sl st. Repeat for 6 rounds. (48 sts each round)
7. **Round 11:** 
   - Ch 1, sc in same st, sc in each st around, join with sl st. (48 sts)

### Edging
The edging adds a nice finish to your adorable hat.

8. **Round 12:** 
   - Ch 1, sc in same st, *sc in next st, ch 1, skip 1 st*, repeat from * to * around, join with sl st. (24 sc)
9. **Round 13:** 
   - Ch 1, sc in each sc around, join with sl st. (24 sts)

## Pom-Pom Attachment
1. **Pom-Pom Attachment:** 
   - Attach one pom-pom to the top of the hat securely using a tapestry needle (optional).

## Finishing Tip: Weave Ends Neatly
Using a tapestry needle, weave in all loose yarn ends securely. Lightly steam block the hat to shape and flatten the brim.

## Beginner Tips
- Use a stitch marker to keep track of your rounds.
- Check your stitch counts at the end of each round to maintain the correct shape.
- Practice maintaining even tension for a beautiful finish.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My hat seems too small!" – This can happen if your tension is too tight. Try using a larger hook or adjusting your tension.
- "The stitches are uneven." – This might be due to varying tension across your work. Focus on keeping a consistent hold on your yarn.
- "I lost track of my rounds!" – It’s easy to lose count. Use a stitch marker to mark the beginning of each round.

## How to Care for Your Baby Pom-Pom Hat
Hand wash in cold water and lay flat to dry. Avoid wringing it out to maintain the hat's shape and softness.
