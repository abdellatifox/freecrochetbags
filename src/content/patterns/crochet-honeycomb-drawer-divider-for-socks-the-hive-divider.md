## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern uses simple stitches like double crochet and single crochet. The clear, step-by-step instructions make it easy to follow along, even if you’re new to crochet.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- dc – double crochet
- sc – single crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- sp – space
- ( ) – work instructions in parentheses the indicated number of times

**Special Stitch - Honeycomb Stitch:** The honeycomb stitch pattern works in alternating rows.  
Row 1: *dc in next st, dc in next sp* (repeat).  
Row 2: *dc in next sp, dc in next st* (repeat). 

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished Item Dimensions:** 30.5 cm × 30.5 cm (12 in × 12 in)
- **Base Height:** 3.8 cm (1.5 in)
- **Total Height (with sides):** 8.9 cm (3.5 in)
- **Gauge:** 14 dc and 7 rows = 10 cm (4 in). Check your gauge by crocheting a swatch of 20 dc and 10 rows, and adjust if necessary.

## Step 1: Making the Base
The base is worked in rows to create a sturdy foundation.

1. **Foundation Chain:** Ch 44.
2. **Row 1:** Dc in 4th ch from hook (counts as first dc), dc in each ch across. (42 dc)
3. **Row 2:** Ch 3 (counts as first dc); *dc in next sp (between dc’s from previous row), dc in next dc*; repeat from * to * across. (42 sts)
4. **Row 3:** Ch 3; *dc in next dc, dc in next sp*; repeat from * to * across. (42 sts)
5. **Row 4-16:** Repeat rows 2 and 3, alternating until you reach 16 rows total.
6. **Final Row:** Ch 1, turn, sl st across to join and finish the base fabric. Fasten off.

## Step 2: Crochet the Sides
Now we’ll add sides for structure.

7. **Foundation for Sides:** Attach Color A to any corner stitch of the base.
8. **Row 1:** Ch 1, sc in same st, sc along the edge of the base (42 sc).
9. **Row 2:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in each stitch around the side edge, join with sl st.
10. **Row 3-5:** Repeat Row 2 two more times (for a total of 3 rows), ensuring to keep the fabric tight at the corners for structure.
11. **Fasten off.**

## Finishing Tip: Weave Ends Neatly
Don't forget to weave in all loose ends using a tapestry needle for a clean finish. You can also block your divider to shape if necessary.

## Beginner Tips
- Keep your workspace organized to avoid losing your yarn and tools.
- Maintain even tension as you crochet to ensure uniform stitches.
- Check your gauge regularly to ensure your piece turns out the right size.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My divider is too small." – Check if your gauge matches the pattern; adjust your hook size if needed.
- "The stitches look uneven." – This may be due to varying tension. Practice maintaining the same tension throughout.
- "The base is curling up." – Ensure you are keeping track of your stitches and rows. Blocking can also help shape it properly.

## How to Care for Your Hive Divider
Hand wash your crochet divider in cold water to keep it looking fresh. Lay it flat to dry to maintain its shape. If you desire extra structural support, consider applying fabric stiffener.
