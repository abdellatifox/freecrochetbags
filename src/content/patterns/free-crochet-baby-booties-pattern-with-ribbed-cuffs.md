This crochet baby booties pattern makes a delightful and cozy pair that features ribbed cuffs, perfect for keeping little feet warm and stylish. The simple stitches used create a cute finish while making it easy for beginners to follow along. 

These booties are great for gifting or keeping your own little ones snuggly. You’ll learn how to work the double crochet stitch as well as the half double crochet, honing your skills while creating something adorable.

- Stylish ribbed cuffs – these add a trendy touch to your baby’s outfit.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern uses basic crochet stitches like double crochet and half double crochet, which are perfect for beginners. Plus, the step-by-step instructions guide you along the way.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sl st – slip stitch
- dc – double crochet
- hdc – half double crochet
- st – stitch
- blo – back loop only

## Size and Gauge
- Finished Bootie Size: approximately 9 cm × 8 cm (3.5 in × 3 in)
- Cuff Height: approximately 5 cm (2 in)
- Gauge: 16 dc and 10 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in double crochet. Make a gauge swatch to ensure the correct fit!

## Step 1: Booties Base
The base is worked in rows.  
1. With Color A, ch 14.  
2. Row 1: dc in 4th ch from hook, dc in next 9 ch, 3 dc in last ch (turning the work), continue dc in remaining 9 ch, join with sl st in top of first dc. (22 sts)  
3. Row 2: ch 3 (this counts as your first dc), dc in next 10 sts, 3 dc in next st, dc in next 10 sts, join with sl st. (26 sts)  
4. Row 3: ch 3, dc in next 11 sts, 3 dc in next st, dc in next 11 sts, join with sl st. (30 sts)  
5. Row 4: ch 3, dc in next 12 sts, 3 dc in next st, dc in next 12 sts, join with sl st. (34 sts)  
6. Row 5: ch 1, hdc in same st, hdc in next 33 sts, join with sl st in top of first hdc. (34 sts)  
7. Cut yarn and fasten off.

## Step 2: Cuff
The cuff is worked in rounds.  
1. Attach Color B with a sl st at the back seam of the bootie.  
2. Round 1: ch 1, hdc in each st around, join with sl st. (34 sts)  
3. Round 2: ch 1, hdc in first st, *hdc blo in next 2 sts,* repeat from * to * around to last st, hdc in last st, join with sl st. (34 sts)  
4. Round 3: ch 1, hdc blo in each st around, join with sl st. (34 sts)  
5. Repeat Round 3 until cuff measures approximately 5 cm (2 in) from the base (about 5 rounds total).  
6. Fasten off and weave in ends.

## How to Finish and Assemble
If desired, sew on buttons for embellishment using a sewing needle and thread. Weave in all ends using the tapestry needle. Block the booties lightly to shape them, if necessary.

## Beginner Tips for Neat Booties
- Keep your stitches counted accurately to maintain sizing.
- Use stitch markers to keep track of rounds if needed.
- Check your gauge to ensure a good fit for little feet.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My booties are too big." – Check if your gauge matches; if not, adjust your hook size to tighten your stitches.
- "The stitches are uneven." – This can happen if you don’t keep consistent tension; practice holding the yarn the same way throughout.
- "I forgot to join my rounds." – Always remember to join each round with a slip stitch to avoid gaps.

## How to Care for Your Booties
Hand wash your booties and lay them flat to dry. Avoid machine washing as it may cause shrinkage.
