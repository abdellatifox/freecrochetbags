This crochet pouf cover pattern makes a stylish and functional addition to your home décor. The Tray Pouf features a flat tray-like top, perfect for placing drinks or decorative items. With basic stitches and advanced techniques like increases and decreases, this project is enjoyable for intermediate crocheters.

You'll learn how to work in rounds and create a cozy cover that fits a round pouf perfectly. This pattern suits anyone looking to elevate their craft skills while adding something unique to their living space.

## Why You'll Love This Tray Pouf
- Stylish and functional – the flat top is great for drinks or as a display area.
- Comfortable and cozy – perfect for lounging in any room of your home.
- Customizable design – change colors or add embellishments like tassels or pom-poms.
- Multi-functional – can be used as extra seating or as a footrest.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! While the project is labeled intermediate, the instructions are clear, and you will only need to understand basic stitches. You'll gain confidence in working with rounds and shaping your project.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- dc – double crochet
- sc – single crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- inc – increase (2 stitches in the same stitch)
- dec – decrease (crochet two stitches together)

## Size and Gauge
- Finished Size: Approximately 51 cm (20 in) in diameter and 30 cm (12 in) in height.
- Gauge: 10 dc and 6 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in single crochet. To check gauge, crochet a swatch of 20 dc x 12 rows. If your gauge does not match, adjust your hook size accordingly.

## Step 1: Pouf Cover Body
The body of the pouf cover is worked in rounds.  
1. **Round 1:** With Natural Beige, ch 4, join with sl st to form a ring. (1 st)  
2. **Round 2:** Ch 2, 11 dc in ring, join with sl st to top of ch 2. (12 sts)  
3. **Round 3:** Ch 2, *inc in next st*, repeat from * around, join with sl st. (24 sts)  
4. **Round 4:** Ch 2, *dc in next st, inc in next st*, repeat from * around, join with sl st. (36 sts)  
5. **Round 5:** Ch 2, *dc in next 2 sts, inc in next st*, repeat from * around, join with sl st. (48 sts)  
6. **Round 6:** Ch 2, *dc in next 3 sts, inc in next st*, repeat from * around, join with sl st. (60 sts)  
7. **Rounds 7–14:** Ch 2, dc in each st around, join with sl st. (60 sts) [8 rounds]  
8. **Round 15:** Ch 2, *dc in next 4 sts, inc in next st*, repeat from * around, join with sl st. (72 sts)  
9. **Rounds 16–23:** Ch 2, dc in each st around, join with sl st. (72 sts) [8 rounds]  
10. **Round 24:** Ch 2, *dc in next 5 sts, inc in next st*, repeat from * around, join with sl st. (84 sts)  
11. **Rounds 25–30:** Ch 2, dc in each st around, join with sl st. (84 sts) [6 rounds]  
12. **Round 31:** Ch 1, sc in each st around, join with sl st. (84 sts)  
13. Fasten off, weave in ends.  

## Step 2: Flat Tray Top
The flat tray top is also worked in rounds.  
1. **Round 1:** With Bottle Green, ch 4, join with sl st to form a ring. (1 st)  
2. **Round 2:** Ch 2, 11 dc in ring, join with sl st. (12 sts)  
3. **Round 3:** Ch 2, *inc in next st*, repeat from * around, join with sl st. (24 sts)  
4. **Round 4:** Ch 2, *dc in next st, inc in next st*, repeat from * around, join with sl st. (36 sts)  
5. **Round 5:** Ch 2, *dc in next 2 sts, inc in next st*, repeat from * around, join with sl st. (48 sts)  
6. **Round 6:** Ch 2, *dc in next 3 sts, inc in next st*, repeat from * around, join with sl st. (60 sts)  
7. **Rounds 7–8:** Ch 2, dc in each st around, join with sl st. (60 sts) [2 rounds]  
8. **Round 9:** Ch 1, sc in each st around, join with sl st. (60 sts)  
9. Fasten off, weave in ends.  

## How to Assemble Your Pouf
1. Place the stuffed pouf insert into the crochet cover.  
2. Align the flat tray top on the top of the pouf and sew it securely using a whip stitch or slip stitch seam.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Tray Pouf
- Keep your stitches even by checking your tension as you go.
- Use stitch markers to keep track of rounds.
- Weave in your ends neatly to give your pouf a polished look.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My pouf is too small." – This could mean your gauge is off. Always check your gauge swatch before starting to ensure proper size.
- "The stitches are uneven." – This usually happens with inconsistent tension. Take a moment to relax and find a comfortable grip on your hook.
- "I can’t get the shape right." – If the pouf is not forming well, check your increases; make sure you're following the pattern for each round.

## How to Care for Your Tray Pouf
To maintain your pouf cover's shape and color, hand wash it and let it air dry. Avoid using bleach or harsh detergents.

## FAQ
### Can I customize the size?

### What yarn can I substitute?
You can use any bulky weight yarn, but note that results may vary based on the yarn's stretch and drape.

### How long will it take to complete?
Depending on your comfort level and speed, the total estimated time for this project is about 6–8 hours.
