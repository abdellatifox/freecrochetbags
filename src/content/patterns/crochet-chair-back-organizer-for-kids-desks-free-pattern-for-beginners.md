This crochet chair back organizer pattern makes a fun and functional project perfect for kids' desks! The Desk Saddle is a great way to keep all those books, supplies, and personal items organized and within reach. We’ll work primarily with the single crochet stitch, making it beginner-friendly.

You’ll learn how to create a practical item while developing your basic crochet skills. This project is perfect for anyone wanting to add a personal touch to a child's workspace.

## Why You'll Love This Chair Back Organizer
- Kid-friendly design – makes organizing fun for your little ones.
- Simple stitches – only uses the single crochet, perfect for beginners.
- Quick project – finish in around 4–6 hours, perfect for a weekend.
- Practical and useful – keeps supplies handy and reduces clutter.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern focuses on basic techniques, making it easy to follow. You'll gain confidence as you work through simple rows and assembly steps.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- blo – back loop only
- sl st – slip stitch
- RS – right side
- WS – wrong side

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished Size:** Approximately 38 × 46 cm (15 × 18 in)
- **Gauge:** 14 sc and 16 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in single crochet.

Check your gauge by making a swatch. If your gauge doesn’t match, try using a larger hook for a looser gauge, or a smaller hook for a tighter gauge.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Body
The body is worked in rows. 
1. **Starting Chain:** Ch 61 using Color A.
2. **Row 1:** Sc in the 2nd ch from the hook and in each ch across. (60 sts)
3. **Row 2:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in each st across. (60 sts)
4. **Repeat Row 2** until the piece measures 46 cm (18 in) tall (about 36 rows total). Cut yarn and fasten off.

### Pocket (Make 2)
Each pocket is made separately.
5. **Starting Chain:** Ch 31 using Color B.
6. **Row 1:** Sc in the 2nd ch from the hook and in each ch across. (30 sts)
7. **Row 2:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in each st across. (30 sts)
8. **Repeat Row 2** until the pocket piece measures 25 cm (10 in) tall (about 20 rows total). Cut yarn and fasten off.

### Assembly of Pockets
We’ll attach the pockets to the body.
9. Position one pocket on the RS of the body, centered, approximately 13 cm (5 in) from the bottom edge. Attach using a sl st around the edges of the pocket. Repeat for the second pocket, placing it 5 cm (2 in) above the first pocket.

### Edging
We will add a decorative border.
10. With Color A, join yarn at the top right corner of the body.
11. **Round 1:** Ch 1, sc evenly around the entire perimeter. Work 3 sc in each corner to round it off. Join with a sl st to the first sc.
12. Fasten off and weave in ends.

## How to Insert the Support
- Cut plastic canvas or cardboard to 35.5 × 43 cm (14 × 17 in) and insert it into the back of the organizer for support.
- Attach Velcro strips to the top of the body on the WS and to corresponding areas on the chair for easy fastening.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Chair Back Organizer
- Keep your yarn tension even to avoid gaps.
- Measure as you go to ensure your pieces are the right size.
- Organize your workspace to keep track of materials and tools.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My organizer is sagging." – This can happen if the gauge is off. Be sure to check your gauge and adjust accordingly.
- "The pockets are too loose." – This may be from crocheting too loosely. Try to maintain consistent tension.
- "It doesn’t fit my chair well." – Always measure your chair before starting. Adjust your starting chain for a better fit.

## How to Care for Your Desk Saddle
Hand wash in cold water and reshape while drying flat to maintain the shape of the organizer. Avoid bleach, and lay flat to dry for best results.

## FAQ
### Can I customize the size?
Yes, for smaller desks, reduce the starting chain by increments of 5 chains for a better fit.

### What if I don’t have the exact colors?
Feel free to use any worsted weight yarn in your favorite colors! Just ensure the fiber content is similar for a consistent outcome.

### How long will this project take?
The total estimated time is approximately 4–6 hours, depending on your skill level and pace.
