This crochet charging station basket pattern makes a wonderful and practical addition to your home, perfect for organizing all your charging cables and devices. By using simple double crochet and single crochet stitches, this project is both enjoyable and beginner-friendly. 

Whether you're looking to tidy up your workspace or create a stylish decor element, you'll find this basket suits your needs perfectly. Plus, you'll learn valuable skills along the way as we work through shaping and adding decorative cord slots together.

## Why You'll Love This Crochet Charging Station Basket
- Functional design – the cord slots allow easy access to your chargers while keeping them organized.
- Quick to make – with an estimated total time of just over 2 hours, you’ll have a useful basket in no time.
- Customizable – use different colors or yarns to match your decor or give the basket a personal touch.
- Beginner-friendly – this pattern uses basic stitches, making it great for crafters at any skill level.

## Skill Level: Easy
You'll need to know the following stitches to complete this pattern:
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
There are no new stitches to learn, so if you’re familiar with these basics, you’re all set!

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- dc – double crochet
- sc – single crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- inc – increase (2 dc in the same stitch)
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- rem – remaining

## Size and Gauge
- Finished size: approx. 25 cm × 12.7 cm (10 in × 5 in)
- Gauge: 12 dc and 7 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in double crochet.

To ensure your basket matches the pattern dimensions, check your gauge by making a small swatch. If your swatch doesn't match, adjust your hook size as needed.

## Step 1: Making the Base
The base is worked in rounds.
1. With Main Color, ch 4, join with sl st to form a ring.  
   (1 st)

2. Round 1: Ch 3 (counts as first dc here and throughout), work 11 dc in the ring. Join with sl st to the top of ch-3.  
   (12 sts)

3. Round 2: Ch 3, dc in same stitch, 2 dc in each st around. Join with sl st.  
   (24 sts)

4. Round 3: Ch 3, dc in same stitch, *dc in next st, 2 dc in next st; repeat from * around. Join with sl st.  
   (36 sts)

5. Round 4: Ch 3, dc in same stitch, *dc in next 2 sts, 2 dc in next st; repeat from * around. Join with sl st.  
   (48 sts)

## Step 2: Crochet the Sides
The sides are also worked in rounds.
6. Round 5: Ch 3, dc in each st around. Join with sl st.  
   (48 sts)

7. Rounds 6–12: Repeat Round 5 six more times. At the end of Round 12, you should have about 12.7 cm (5 in) of height, measured vertically from the base.  
   (48 sts each round)

## Step 3: Add the Cord Slots
8. Round 13: Ch 1, sc in same stitch, *ch 4, skip 4 sts, sc in next st; repeat from * around. Join with sl st.  
   (12 slots made)

9. Round 14: Ch 3, 3 dc in each ch-4 space around, join with sl st.  
   (36 dc)

## Final Edging
10. Round 15: Ch 1, sc in each dc around. Join with sl st.  
   (36 sts)

11. Round 16: Ch 1, sc in same stitch, *sc in next 3 sts, inc in next st; repeat from * around. Join with sl st.  
   (48 sts)

## Finishing Tip: Weave Ends Neatly
Weave in all ends using the tapestry needle. Block the basket to shape it evenly, pinning down the edges as needed. Let it dry completely.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Basket
- Stay organized – keep your yarn, hook, and supplies together to simplify your process.
- Check your gauge – measuring ensures your basket will turn out the correct size.
- Maintain even tension – practice keeping your stitches consistent for a neat finish.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My basket is too small. – You may have missed adjusting your hook size to meet gauge. Check your swatch.
- The stitches are uneven. – This often happens with inconsistent tension. Focus on keeping your grip steady.
- I can’t see the cord slots clearly. – Ensure you’re following the slot instructions correctly, matching the sc and ch counts as stated.

## How to Care for Your Basket
To keep your basket looking great, hand wash it in cold water and lay it flat to dry. Avoid wringing or twisting to maintain its shape.

### FAQ
### Can I customize the size?
Yes! You can add extra rounds to the sides for a larger basket or use a lighter-weight yarn to adjust the dimensions.

### What type of yarn can I use?
Look for worsted weight cotton yarns. You can choose various colors or effects, like variegated yarns for a unique look.

### How long will it take to make this basket?
On average, allow about 2 hours and 15 minutes to complete this project, depending on your crochet speed.
