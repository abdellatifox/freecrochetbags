This crochet basket pattern makes a charming, textured toy basket that’s perfect for storing toys or serving as a decorative accent in any child's room. It uses the basket weave stitch, which creates a lovely woven appearance, offering a fun and enjoyable challenge for your crochet skills. 

This project suits anyone looking to expand their skills beyond the basics. You'll learn new stitches as well as how to create a sturdy, functional basket that enhances your home decor.

## Why You'll Love This Toy Basket
- Cozy and decorative – great for organizing toys or adding a touch of charm to a child's room.
- Functional and sturdy – made to hold toys, books, or other items with style.
- Easy to follow – with step-by-step instructions, it's beginner-friendly, even for those tackling new stitches.
- Customizable size – adjust the height and colors to perfectly fit your space and preferences.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! While this pattern involves some intermediate techniques, the clear instructions and repetition of rounds make it accessible. Don’t worry if you’re new to the basket weave stitch; you'll master it with a little practice!

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sl st – slip stitch
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- [ ] – work instructions within brackets into the same stitch or space.

**Basket Weave Stitch:**
1. Rows will alternate between a solid row of single crochet (sc) and a row of double crochet (dc) in clusters.
2. The pattern creates the illusion of woven fabric by alternating the direction of the stitches.

## Gauge and Finished Size
- Gauge: 12 stitches and 10 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in basket weave pattern.
- Finished dimensions: Approximately 30 cm in diameter and 20 cm in height (12 in × 8 in).

## Step 1: Making the Base
The base is worked in rounds. 
1. Using Color A, ch 4.
2. Round 1: Work 11 dc in the 4th ch from hook (first dc counts as 1), join with sl st to top of first dc. (12 dc)
3. Round 2: Ch 2, *dc in next dc, 2 dc in next dc* repeat around, join with sl st. (18 dc)
4. Round 3: Ch 2, *dc in next 2 dc, 2 dc in next dc* repeat around, join with sl st. (24 dc)
5. Round 4: Ch 2, *dc in next 3 dc, 2 dc in next dc* repeat around, join with sl st. (30 dc)
6. Round 5: Ch 2, *dc in next 4 dc, 2 dc in next dc* repeat around, join with sl st. (36 dc)
7. Round 6: Ch 2, *dc in next 5 dc, 2 dc in next dc* repeat around, join with sl st. (42 dc)

## Step 2: Crochet the Sides
The sides continue upwards from the base. 
8. Round 7: Ch 1, sc in each dc around, join with sl st. (42 sc)
9. Round 8: Ch 2, *dc in next 6 sc, 2 dc in next sc* repeat around, join with sl st. (49 dc)
10. Round 9: Ch 1, sc in each dc around, join with sl st. (49 sc)
11. Round 10: Ch 2, *dc in next 7 sc, 2 dc in next sc* repeat around, join with sl st. (56 dc)
12. Rounds 11–13: Repeat rounds 9 and 10 for a total of 5 sc rounds and 5 dc rounds.

## Step 3: Finish the Edges
13. Round 14: Ch 1, sc in each dc around, join with sl st. (56 sc)
14. Rounds 15–18: Repeat the alternating pattern as established from rounds 9 and 10 until reaching your desired basket height (approximately 20 cm or 8 in).
15. Final Round: Ch 1, sc in each stitch around, join with sl st. Fasten off and weave in ends.

## Finishing Tip: Weave Ends Neatly
There is no assembly required as this is a continuous pattern. Optionally, you can add a decorative edge by working a round of crab stitch (reverse sc) around the top to give it a neat finish. Weave in any remaining ends with a tapestry needle.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Toy Basket
- Keep your workspace organized, so you easily track your yarn and tools.
- Maintain even tension as you work for a more uniform basket.
- Regularly check your size against the measurements to ensure you're on the right track.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My basket is too small." – This could be due to skipping stitches. Count your stitches per round to ensure accuracy.
- "The stitches are uneven." – This may happen if your tension varies. Try to keep the same pressure as you crochet.
- "The basket looks wobbly." – This can be caused by not following the stitch counts. Always double-check after each round.

## How to Care for Your Toy Basket
For best results, hand wash in cold water and lay the basket flat to dry. This will help maintain its shape and cozy texture.

### FAQ
### Can I customize the size?
Absolutely! You can adjust the height by adding or removing rounds. Keep the base the same for the diameter.

### What if I want a different color?
Feel free to experiment with any colors you like! Just make sure you have enough yarn for your basket.
