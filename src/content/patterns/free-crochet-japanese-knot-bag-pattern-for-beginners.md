This crochet Japanese Knot Bag pattern makes a stylish and functional accessory for your daily use. The unique twist design and chunky yarn create a sturdy yet soft bag, perfect for carrying your essentials. You'll find it easy and enjoyable to make, using basic stitches like single crochet.

This project is suitable for all skill levels, especially beginners, as it guides you through straightforward steps while helping you practice your crochet skills.

## Why You'll Love This Japanese Knot Bag
- Stylish and unique design – the twist adds interest and flair to your bag.
- Versatile for various occasions – perfect for everyday use or as a fashion statement.
- Quick to make with chunky yarn – finish in about 3 hours!

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern uses basic stitches and simple assembly techniques, so it's perfect for beginners.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- st(s) – stitch(es)

## Size and Gauge
- Finished size: 30 × 25 cm (12 × 10 in) with a base of 8 cm (3 in).
- Gauge: 8 sc and 6 rows = 10 cm (4 in).

## Step 1: Body of the Bag (Make 2)
The body of the bag is made of two rectangular pieces. 
1. Foundation Row: With Color A, ch 36.
2. Row 1: Sc in 2nd ch from hook and in each ch across. (35 sts)
3. Row 2: Ch 1, turn. Sc in each st across. (35 sts)
4. Repeat Row 2 until the piece measures 25 cm (10 in) tall, approximately 15 rows total.
5. Fasten off Color A. (You'll have 2 pieces of the same size.)

## Step 2: Base of the Bag
The base provides stability to your bag. 
6. With Color B, ch 24.
7. Row 1: Sc in 2nd ch from hook and each ch across. (23 sts)
8. Row 2: Ch 1, turn. Sc in each st across. (23 sts)
9. Repeat Row 2 until the piece measures 30 cm (12 in) long, approximately 10 rows total.
10. Fasten off Color B.

## Step 3: Assembly
Now we’ll bring everything together.
11. Place the two rectangular body pieces with Wrong sides facing each other.
12. Pin the base piece to the bottom edge of the bag body. Using Color A, sc around the edges to join the body and base together. (35 + 23 + 35 + 23 = 116 sts)
13. Fasten off and weave in ends.

## Step 4: Finishing and Strap
Let’s finish your bag with the straps.
14. For the straps, ch 50 using Color A.
15. Sc in each ch across. Fasten off and repeat for the second strap.
16. Attach one strap to one side of the bag and the other strap to the opposite side by using sl st to secure them in place.
17. Weave in all ends.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Japanese Knot Bag
- Keep tension even throughout your work for a polished look.
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rows if you find it helpful.
- Check your gauge before starting to ensure a perfect fit.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My bag is too small. – Check your gauge and adjust your hook size if needed.
- The stitches are uneven. – Make sure your tension remains consistent throughout the project.
- The base feels floppy. – You may need to crochet tighter or add an additional row for sturdiness.

## How to Care for Your Japanese Knot Bag
To keep your bag looking great, hand wash in cold water and lay it flat to dry. This will help maintain its shape and color.

## FAQ
### Can I customize the size?
Yes! This bag can be adjusted by making the body or base pieces longer or shorter while maintaining the stitch count.

### What yarn can I use?
You can substitute with any bulky yarn (Category 5), but be sure to check the gauge for proper fit and structure.

### How long will this take me to make?
Around 3 hours, depending on your crochet speed and experience level. Most of the time is spent crocheting the main components.
