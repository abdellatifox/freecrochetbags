## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- dc – double crochet
- fpdc – front post double crochet
- bpdc – back post double crochet
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- rep – repeat

## How to Make a Waffle Stitch
1. Start with a foundation chain of an even number of stitches.
2. In the first row, double crochet across.
3. Alternate front post double crochet and back post double crochet in subsequent rows.

## Size and Gauge
- Finished Size: Approximately 61 × 91 cm (24 × 36 in)
- Gauge: 10 sts and 6 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in waffle stitch pattern.

To check your gauge, make a small swatch and measure the central 10 cm (4 in) both horizontally and vertically.

## Step 1: The Body of the Mat
The body of the mat is worked in rows. 
1. Foundation Chain: Ch 62 for the desired width of 61 cm (24 in).
2. Row 1: Dc in the 4th ch from the hook and in each ch across. (60 dc)
3. Row 2: Ch 2 (this counts as the first dc), turn. [*fpdc in next st, bpdc in next st*] across until the last st. (60 sts)
4. Row 3: Ch 2, turn. [*bpdc in next st, fpdc in next st*] across until the last st. (60 sts)
5. Row 4: Repeat Row 2. (60 sts)
6. Row 5: Repeat Row 3. (60 sts)
7. Continue: Repeat Rows 2 and 3 until the mat reaches approximately 91 cm (36 in) long (about 27 total rows from the foundation row).
8. Final Row: Ch 2, turn. Work 1 row of dc across to finish. (60 dc)

## How to Weave in Ends
1. Use a tapestry needle to weave in the loose ends from the start and finish of the mat.
2. Ensure everything is secured and trimmed neatly.

## Finishing Tip: Block the Mat
Lightly steam block the mat if desired. This can help straighten the edges and enhance the lovely texture of the waffle stitch.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Bath Mat
- Stay organized – keep your workspace tidy to avoid losing stitches or tools.
- Maintain even tension – this ensures a consistent look in your stitches.
- Check size regularly – measuring as you go helps confirm your mat will be the right dimensions.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My mat feels too small. – This might be due to an uneven foundation chain. Double-check to make sure you have the right number of stitches.
- The texture isn't showing up. – Ensure you are alternating correctly between fpdc and bpdc. Review the waffle stitch instructions if needed.
- My stitches look uneven. – This often happens with varying tension. Take a moment to relax and keep your grip steady as you crochet.

## How to Care for Your Bath Mat
Hand wash your mat in cold water and lay it flat to dry. This will help maintain the integrity of the cotton fabric and ensure it lasts for many cozy showers to come.

### FAQ
### Can I customize the size?
Yes! You can adjust the width and length by changing the foundation chain and the number of rows you work.

### What if my gauge doesn't match?
If your gauge is off, try using a larger hook if you have too few stitches, or a smaller hook if you have too many.

### How long does it take to make?
The estimated time to complete the mat is approximately 5–7 hours, depending on your skill level and speed.
