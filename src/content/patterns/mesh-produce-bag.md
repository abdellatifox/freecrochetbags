This **crochet mesh produce bag** is a small project with a big impact. It's light, stretchy and perfect for carrying fruit, vegetables or bread home from the market – no plastic needed.

It's a quick make (about two hours), so you can crochet a whole set in a weekend.

## Why You'll Love This Produce Bag

- **Reusable and washable** – better for the planet.
- **Stretches to fit** a surprising amount.
- **Quick** – one bag in an evening.
- **Great practice** for working in the round.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)

- **ch** – chain
- **sl st** – slip stitch
- **sc** – single crochet
- **dc** – double crochet
- **magic ring** – an adjustable loop you can pull closed.
- **loop** – in this pattern, the ch-5 arches of the mesh.

## Size

Finished bag: about 22 cm wide × 30 cm tall, before stretching.

## Step 1: The Round Base

Make a magic ring.

**Round 1:** Ch 3 (counts as a dc), 11 dc in the ring. Pull the ring closed and join to the top of the ch-3. **(12 dc)**

**Round 2:** Ch 3, dc in the same stitch, 2 dc in each stitch around. Join. **(24 dc)**

**Round 3:** Ch 3, 2 dc in the next stitch, *dc in the next stitch, 2 dc in the next stitch.* Repeat around. Join. **(36 dc)**

**Round 4:** Ch 3, dc in the next stitch, 2 dc in the next stitch, *dc in the next 2 stitches, 2 dc in the next stitch.* Repeat around. Join. **(48 dc)**

## Step 2: The Mesh

**Round 5:** *Ch 5, skip 2 stitches, sc in the next stitch.* Repeat from * around. **(16 loops)**

**Round 6 and onward:** Sl st into the first 3 chains of the first loop to reach its middle. *Ch 5, sc in the middle of the next loop.* Repeat from *. Don't join – keep going round and round in a spiral.

Move your stitch marker up every round. Continue until the bag measures about **30 cm** tall.

> **Why a spiral?** Working without joining makes the mesh look even, with no visible "seam" line.

## Step 3: The Top and Drawstring

**Top Round 1:** Work 3 sc in each loop around. **(48 sc)**

**Top Round 2:** Sc in each stitch around.

**Top Round 3 (eyelets):** *Sc in the next 2 stitches, ch 2, skip 2 stitches.* Repeat around. **(12 holes)**

**Top Round 4:** Sc in each sc and 2 sc in each ch-2 space. Join and fasten off.

**Drawstring:** Ch 150. Sl st in the 2nd ch from the hook and in each ch back along the chain. Weave it through the eyelet holes and tie the ends together.

## Beginner Tips

- **Keep your mesh loops the same size** – try to pull each chain to the same height.
- **Cotton yarn works best** because it's strong and washable.
- **Make a set of three** in different colors for fruit, veg and bread.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)

- **My base is wavy.** Check your stitch counts – you may have extra increases.
- **My base is curling up.** Try a slightly bigger hook for the base.
- **The mesh is twisting.** That's normal for a spiral. It straightens out when the bag is filled.

## FAQ

### What yarn is best for a crochet produce bag?

100% cotton or a cotton-linen blend. It's strong, food-safe and easy to wash.

### Can I wash my produce bag?

Yes. Wash it by hand or on a gentle machine cycle, then hang to dry.

### How do I make the bag bigger?

Add another increase round to the base (60 stitches, 20 loops) and keep going until it's as tall as you like.
