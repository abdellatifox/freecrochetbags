## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sl st – slip stitch
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- st(s) – stitch(es)

## Size and Gauge
- Finished size: approximately 25 cm (10 in) wide and 20 cm (8 in) tall
- Gauge: 12 dc and 8 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in double crochet. It's a good idea to check your gauge to ensure your basket turns out the right size.

## Step 1: Making the Basket Body
The basket body is worked in rounds.

1. **Foundation Round:** Ch 4, join with a sl st to form a ring. (1 st)
2. **Round 1:** Ch 2 (counts as first dc), work 11 dc into the ring, join with sl st to top of ch 2. (12 st)
3. **Round 2:** Ch 2, 2 dc in each st around, join with sl st to top of ch 2. (24 st)
4. **Round 3:** Ch 2, *dc in next st, 2 dc in next st* around, join. (36 st)
5. **Round 4:** Ch 2, *dc in next 2 st, 2 dc in next st* around, join. (48 st)
6. **Rounds 5–10:** Ch 2, dc in each st around, join. (48 st) [Repeat for 6 rounds.]
7. **Round 11:** Switch to Contrast color. Ch 1, sc in each st around, join. (48 st)
8. **Round 12:** Ch 1, sc in next 3 st, *ch 5, skip 2 st, sc in next 3 st* around, join. (16 ch-5 spaces)

## Step 2: Crochet the Strap
The strap is made separately and then attached to the basket.

1. **Strap Foundation:** Ch 30, sc in 2nd ch from hook and across (29 sc).
2. **Strap Rounds:** Ch 1, sc in each st for 3 rows. (29 st)
3. **Attach Strap to Basket:** Place the strap on the basket's edge. Join with a sl st to the basket top edge, ensuring it's parallel to the basket.

## Step 3: Finish the Assembly
1. **Clip Attachment:** Sew or attach the clip to the strap securely.
2. **Reinforcement (optional):** Cut stiff cardboard to size (10 x 10 inches) and place inside the bottom of the basket for added stability.

## Beginner Tips
- Keep your yarn and tools organized as you work to avoid any loss or confusion.
- Maintain an even tension to ensure your basket retains its shape.
- Check your size frequently to make sure your basket is growing correctly.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My basket is too small." – This might be due to tight stitches; check your tension and gauge.
- "The stitches are uneven." – This can happen if you’re not maintaining consistent tension; practice smaller swatches before working on your project.
- "I can’t figure out how to change colors." – Make sure to switch yarns at the end of a round with a slip stitch; it’ll help you keep your colors clean and not tangled.

## How to Care for Your Crochet Odd Sock Basket
For best results, machine wash your basket in cold water and lay it flat to dry. Avoid bleach to keep your colors vibrant and fresh!

### FAQ
### Can I customize the size?
Absolutely! You can make a larger basket by repeating the steps in Round 6 for more rounds before starting the strap.

### What if I don't have the recommended yarn?
You can substitute with any worsted weight cotton yarn. Just make sure your gauge matches to keep the basket size consistent.

### How long will this project take?
The estimated total time is about 4.5 hours, divided into approximately 3 hours for the body, 1 hour for the strap, and 30 minutes for assembly.
