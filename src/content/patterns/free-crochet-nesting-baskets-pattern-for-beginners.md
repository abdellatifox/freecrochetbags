## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern uses simple stitches and clear instructions. You'll practice your chaining, single crochet, and double crochet, which are foundational skills in crochet.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- **ch** – chain
- **sc** – single crochet
- **dc** – double crochet
- **sl st** – slip stitch
- **inc** – increase (2 stitches in the same stitch)

## Size and Gauge
- **Small Basket:** 15 cm (6 in) diameter, 10 cm (4 in) tall
- **Medium Basket:** 20 cm (8 in) diameter, 12.5 cm (5 in) tall
- **Large Basket:** 25 cm (10 in) diameter, 15 cm (6 in) tall
- **Gauge:** 12 dc and 7 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in diameter in double crochet. 

Be sure to check your gauge; if it doesn’t match, try using a different hook size.

## Step 1: Small Basket
The small basket is created using rounds.

1. **Foundation Row:** Using size H hook and Natural Beige, ch 5, sl st in the first ch to form a ring.
2. **Round 1:** Ch 2 (counts as first dc), 11 dc in the ring. Join with sl st to the top of the beginning ch-2. (12 sts)
3. **Round 2:** Ch 2, 1 dc in the same st, inc in each st around. Join with sl st. (24 sts)
4. **Round 3:** Ch 2, dc in the same st, *1 dc in next st, inc in next st; repeat from * around. Join with sl st. (36 sts)
5. **Rounds 4–8:** Ch 2, dc in each st around. Join with sl st. Repeat for 5 rounds. (36 sts each round)
6. **Finish:** Fasten off, weave in ends.

## Step 2: Medium Basket
Follow the same method for the medium basket, just use a larger foundation chain.

1. **Foundation Row:** Using size H hook and Natural Beige, ch 6, sl st in the first ch to form a ring.
2. **Round 1:** Ch 2, 11 dc in the ring. Join with sl st. (12 sts)
3. **Round 2:** Ch 2, 1 dc in the same st, inc in each st around. Join with sl st. (24 sts)
4. **Round 3:** Ch 2, dc in the same st, *1 dc in next st, inc in next st; repeat from * around. Join with sl st. (36 sts)
5. **Rounds 4–9:** Ch 2, dc in each st around. Join with sl st. Repeat for 6 rounds. (36 sts each round)
6. **Finish:** Fasten off, weave in ends.

## Step 3: Large Basket
For the large basket, repeat the same technique but with an even larger foundation.

1. **Foundation Row:** Using size H hook and Natural Beige, ch 7, sl st in the first ch to form a ring.
2. **Round 1:** Ch 2, 11 dc in the ring. Join with sl st. (12 sts)
3. **Round 2:** Ch 2, 1 dc in the same st, inc in each st around. Join with sl st. (24 sts)
4. **Round 3:** Ch 2, dc in the same st, *1 dc in next st, inc in next st; repeat from * around. Join with sl st. (36 sts)
5. **Rounds 4–10:** Ch 2, dc in each st around. Join with sl st. Repeat for 7 rounds. (36 sts each round)
6. **Finish:** Fasten off, weave in ends.

## Finishing Tip: Weave Ends Neatly
No assembly is needed since each basket is completed individually. You can embellish them further if you’d like!

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Nesting Basket
- Keep your workspace organized to avoid losing your materials. 
- Maintain even tension while crocheting to ensure uniformity.
- Count your stitches at the end of each round to avoid mistakes. 

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My basket is too small." – You may have missed a stitch or not followed the count. Always check your rounds carefully.
- "The stitches are uneven." – This could be due to varying tension. Practice keeping an even tension on your yarn.
- "I have a hole in my basket." – You might not have joined tightly or skipped stitches. Make sure to check your work at each round's end.

## How to Care for Your Nesting Baskets
Hand wash your baskets in cold water and lay them flat to dry. Avoid wringing to maintain their shape and integrity.
