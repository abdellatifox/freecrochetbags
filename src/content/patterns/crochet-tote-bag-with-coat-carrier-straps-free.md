The Coat Hitch Tote is perfect for carrying your daily essentials, and it’s enjoyable to make, using simple stitches like single and double crochet throughout. You'll learn to create a sturdy bag that you can use every day.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! While the pattern is marked as intermediate, beginners can certainly give it a try, especially with the clear step-by-step instructions. You'll learn how to crochet the body, create a base, and assemble the tote, gaining confidence along the way.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- st – stitch
- sl st – slip stitch
- sp – space
- DC Bobble – yarn over, insert hook into indicated st, yarn over and pull up a loop, (yarn over, pull through 2 loops) two times, yarn over and pull through remaining loops on hook.

## Size and Gauge
- Gauge: 12 dc and 8 rows = 10 cm (4 in).
- Finished Size: 35 × 25 cm (14 × 10 in) wide and tall, 12.5 cm (5 in) deep.

## Step 1: Body of the Tote
The body is worked in rows.  
1. Foundation Chain: Ch 52.  
2. Row 1: Sc in 2nd ch from hook and in each ch across (51 sc).  
3. Row 2: Ch 3 (this counts as your first dc), turn, dc in each st across (51 dc).  
4. Row 3: Ch 1, turn, sc in each st across (51 sc).  
5. Row 4: Ch 3, turn, dc in each st across (51 dc).  
6. Repeat Rows 3 & 4 until piece measures 25 cm (10 in) tall (12 total rows).  
7. Fasten off.

## Step 2: Base of the Tote
The base is created separately in rows.  
1. Ch 12.  
2. Row 1: Sc in 2nd ch from hook and in each ch across (11 sc).  
3. Rows 2–5: Ch 1, turn, sc in each st across (11 sc).  
4. Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing.

## Step 3: Assembling the Tote
1. With the wrong sides together, sew the base to the bottom edge of the body using the tail from the base.  
2. Pin and sew the side seams together to form a rectangle, leaving the top open.

## Step 4: Coat Carrier Straps
The straps are made in rows using the contrast color.  
1. Ch 25.  
2. Row 1: Sc in 2nd ch from hook and in each ch across (24 sc).  
3. Rows 2–4: Ch 1, turn, sc in each st across (24 sc).  
4. Fasten off.  
5. Make 2 pieces for straps.

## Step 5: Attaching Straps
1. Position each strap 5 cm (2 in) in from the side seam, aligning them with the top edge of the tote.  
2. Sew each strap securely using the tapestry needle.  
3. Attach a button at the base of each strap as a decorative feature.

## Beginner Tips
- Keep your workspace organized – gather all your materials before you start.
- Maintain even tension – practice your stitches to ensure uniformity.
- Check your size as you go – measure your work frequently to achieve the desired dimensions.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My bag is too small. – This may happen if your gauge is off. Always check your swatch to ensure accuracy.
- The stitches are uneven. – This can be a result of inconsistent tension. Try to relax your grip and focus on keeping your yarn tension uniform.
- I can’t keep track of the rows. – Use stitch markers to help mark the beginning of each row or keep count of your rows as you crochet.

## How to Care for Your Tote Bag
To keep your tote looking its best, hand wash or machine wash on a gentle cycle. Lay flat to dry, avoiding wringing to maintain its shape. 

## FAQ
### Can I customize the size?
Absolutely! You can adjust the foundation chain length and the number of rows to create a larger or smaller tote. Just ensure that the proportions remain balanced.

### What can I use instead of cotton yarn?
You can substitute with other worsted weight yarns, such as acrylic or linen blends, for different textures and care options. Just be aware that the drape and durability may change.

### How long does it take to make this bag?
This tote bag can be completed in about 6 hours, depending on your skill level and pace.
