Few things are as sweet as a pair of tiny handmade booties. This **easy crochet baby booties pattern** makes soft, slip-on booties for newborns with a folded cuff that helps them stay on little feet.

They're made in one piece, from the sole up, using only single crochet and half double crochet.

## Why You'll Love These Baby Booties

- **One piece** – no sewing the sole to the top.
- **Only basic stitches** – sc, sc2tog and hdc.
- **Quick** – about one hour per bootie.
- **A perfect gift** for a baby shower.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)

- **ch** – chain
- **sl st** – slip stitch
- **sc** – single crochet
- **hdc** – half double crochet
- **inc** – increase: 2 sc in the same stitch.
- **sc2tog** – single crochet two together (a decrease): insert hook in the next stitch and pull up a loop, insert hook in the following stitch and pull up a loop, yarn over and pull through all 3 loops.
- **BLO** – back loop only.

## Size

Newborn to 3 months – sole length about 9 cm.

## Step 1: The Sole

Ch 10.

**Round 1:** Sc in the 2nd ch from the hook, sc in the next 7 ch, 3 sc in the last ch. Turn your work to crochet along the other side of the chain: sc in the next 7 ch, 2 sc in the last ch. Join with a sl st to the first sc. **(20 sc)**

**Round 2:** Ch 1, inc, sc 7, inc 3 times, sc 7, inc 2 times. Join. **(26 sc)**

**Round 3:** Ch 1, sc 1, inc, sc 7, (sc 1, inc) 3 times, sc 7, (sc 1, inc) 2 times. Join. **(32 sc)**

> **Tip:** the sole should lie flat and look like a little oval. The end where you made 3 sc in Round 1 is the **toe**. Place a stitch marker in the middle stitch of the toe end.

## Step 2: The Sides

**Round 4:** Ch 1, sc in the **BLO** of each stitch around. Join. **(32 sc)** This creates a neat edge where the sole turns upward.

**Rounds 5–6:** Ch 1, sc in each stitch around. Join. **(32 sc)**

## Step 3: Shape the Toe

**Round 7:** Ch 1, sc until you are 6 stitches before the toe marker, sc2tog 6 times, sc to the end. Join. **(26 sc)**

**Round 8:** Ch 1, sc until you are 3 stitches before the toe center, sc2tog 3 times, sc to the end. Join. **(23 sc)**

## Step 4: The Cuff

**Rounds 9–13:** Ch 1, hdc in the **BLO** of each stitch around. Join. **(23 hdc)**

Fasten off and weave in the ends. Fold the cuff down. Make a second bootie the same way.

> **Safety note:** avoid buttons, beads or small decorations on booties for newborns – they can come loose.

## Beginner Tips

- **Count every round** – the numbers in brackets tell you what you should have.
- **Use a soft, machine-washable yarn** that isn't fuzzy.
- **Make both booties at the same time** (one round on each) so they match.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)

- **The sole is cupping.** Try a slightly bigger hook for the sole only.
- **The toe looks pointy.** Spread your decreases evenly across the front.
- **The bootie falls off.** Add one more cuff round, or thread a ribbon through Round 9 and tie it loosely.

## FAQ

### What size are newborn baby booties?

Newborn booties usually have a sole length of 8–9 cm. This pattern makes a 9 cm sole.

### What yarn is best for baby booties?

A soft DK cotton, bamboo or baby acrylic. Choose something machine washable and non-fuzzy.

### How do I make bigger baby booties?

Start with a longer chain (ch 12 for 3–6 months) and add 2 stitches to every stitch count.
