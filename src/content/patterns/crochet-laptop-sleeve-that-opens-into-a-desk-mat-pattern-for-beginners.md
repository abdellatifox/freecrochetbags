This crochet laptop sleeve pattern makes a versatile accessory that cleverly transforms into a flat desk mat. It’s designed to protect your laptop while providing a comfortable surface for work or study on the go. We’ll be using basic double crochet and single crochet stitches, making it an enjoyable project to tackle.

This pattern is perfect for those looking to enhance their crochet skills while creating something functional. You'll learn assembly techniques and how to adjust the size for your laptop needs.

## Why You'll Love This Laptop Sleeve
- Practical and versatile – it doubles as a protective sleeve and a workspace mat.
- Stylish design – customize your colors to fit your personal style or workspace.
- Great for on-the-go – perfect for students or professionals who work remotely.
- Quick to make – with an estimated total time of only 9 hours, you’ll have a useful item in no time.

## Skill Level: Intermediate
To complete this project, you should be familiar with basic stitches like chain, single crochet, and double crochet. You will learn how to work in the front and back loops, which adds a fun twist to your crochet skills.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- DC – double crochet
- SC – single crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- FLO – front loop only
- BLO – back loop only

## Size and Gauge
- Finished Laptop Sleeve: 15” x 10” (38 cm x 25 cm)
- Finished Desk Mat: 30” x 20” (76 cm x 50 cm)
- Gauge: 14 DC and 8 rows = 4 inches (10 cm) in double crochet. Make a swatch first to ensure your gauge matches.

## Step 1: Crocheting the Laptop Sleeve
The laptop sleeve is made in rows.  
1. **Foundation Row:** Ch 40. (39 sts)  
2. **Row 1:** DC in the 4th ch from the hook and in each ch across. (37 DC)  
3. **Row 2:** Ch 3 (counts as DC), turn, DC in each DC across. (37 DC)  
4. **Rows 3–20:** Repeat Row 2 for 18 more rows. (37 DC)  
5. **Row 21:** Ch 1, turn, SC in each DC across. (37 SC)  
6. **Row 22:** Ch 3, turn, DC in each SC across. (37 DC)  
7. **Row 23:** Ch 1, turn, SC in each DC across. (37 SC)  
8. **Row 24:** Ch 3, turn, DC in each SC across. (37 DC)  
9. **Row 25:** Ch 1, turn, SC in each DC across. (37 SC)  
10. **Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing.**

## Step 2: Creating the Desk Mat
The desk mat is made in a similar way to the sleeve but will be larger.  
1. **Beginning Row:** With accent color (AC), ch 80. (79 sts)  
2. **Row 1:** DC in the 4th ch from the hook, DC in each ch across. (77 DC)  
3. **Row 2:** Ch 3, turn, DC in each DC across. (77 DC)  
4. **Rows 3–35:** Repeat Row 2 for 33 more rows. (77 DC)  
5. **Last Row:** Ch 1, SC in each DC across. (77 SC)  
6. **Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing.**

## Step 3: Assembly
Now it's time to put it all together!  
1. Fold the sleeve in half, with the wrong side facing out, and sew the sides together using a whip stitch or slip stitch, leaving the top open.  
2. Fold the mat in half, pin to secure it, and stitch along the edge to form a long rectangle.  
3. Attach the sleeve to one side of the mat by sewing it in place or using a few strong, decorative buttons to make it removable.

## Step 4: Finishing Touches
1. Weave in all ends with a tapestry needle.  
2. Optionally, sew the felt lining into the inside of the laptop sleeve for extra protection.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Laptop Sleeve
- Keep your workspace tidy to avoid losing track of stitches and materials.
- Maintain an even tension in your stitches for a smooth finish.
- Measure your work regularly to ensure it matches the expected sizes.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My laptop sleeve is too small." – You may need to adjust your initial chain length and row count. Don’t worry! Just make it longer next time.
- "The stitches are uneven." – This might be due to inconsistent tension. Try to keep your grip steady on the yarn as you work.
- "The mat doesn’t lay flat." – This can happen if the rows are not even. Check your counting and row repetitions to keep it even.

## How to Care for Your Laptop Sleeve
To keep your laptop sleeve looking great, hand wash it with mild detergent. Lay it flat to dry and avoid wringing out the material to maintain its shape.

### FAQ
### Can I customize the size?
Yes! You can easily adjust the initial chain length for both the laptop sleeve and desk mat to fit your specific laptop size.

### What yarn can I substitute?
You can use other worsted weight cotton yarns in your favorite colors. Just check the weight to ensure a good fit.

### How long will it take to make this?
The total estimated time is about 9 hours, with approximately 4 hours for the sleeve and 5 for the desk mat.
