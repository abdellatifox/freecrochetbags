This **waffle stitch beanie crochet pattern** makes a thick, squishy hat with a grid texture that looks just like a waffle. It looks complicated, but it's made from only two stitches: the double crochet and the front post double crochet.

If you've made a simple hat or scarf before, this is a great next step. We'll walk through the new stitch slowly.

## Why You'll Love This Waffle Stitch Beanie

- **Extra warm** – the waffle texture traps warm air.
- **Stretchy ribbed brim** that stays in place.
- **No complicated shaping** – the top is gathered closed.
- **Three adult sizes** so it fits just right.

## Skill Level: Confident Beginner

You should already know how to chain, single crochet (sc), half double crochet (hdc) and double crochet (dc). The only new stitch is the **front post double crochet**, explained below.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)

- **ch** – chain
- **sl st** – slip stitch
- **sc** – single crochet
- **hdc** – half double crochet
- **dc** – double crochet
- **BLO** – back loop only: work into the loop farthest from you instead of both loops.
- **FPdc** – front post double crochet: yarn over, and instead of going into the top of the stitch, push your hook **from front to back to front around the post** (the tall body) of the stitch. Finish like a normal dc. This makes the stitch pop forward.

## Sizes and Gauge

- **Sizes:** Adult S (M, L) – fits a head of 51 (56, 60) cm.
- **Gauge:** 14 dc and 8 rows = 10 cm.

To measure your head, wrap a tape measure around your forehead and just above your ears.

## Step 1: Make the Ribbed Brim

The brim is worked flat in rows, then joined into a circle.

Ch 10.

**Row 1:** Hdc in the 3rd ch from the hook and in each ch across. Turn. (8 hdc)

**Row 2:** Ch 1 (this does not count as a stitch), hdc in the **BLO** of each stitch across. Turn.

Repeat Row 2 until the band fits snugly around your head when gently stretched – usually **40 (44, 48) rows**.

Fold the band so the short ends meet and sl st them together. Turn it right side out.

## Step 2: Crochet the Waffle Stitch Body

Now we work in rounds along one long edge of the brim. We do not turn.

**Round 1:** Ch 1 and work **66 (72, 78) sc** evenly around the edge of the brim (about 3 sc for every 2 rows). Join with a sl st. The number must divide by 3.

**Round 2:** Ch 2 (does not count as a stitch), dc in each stitch around. Join.

**Round 3:** Ch 2, *FPdc around the next stitch, dc in the next 2 stitches.* Repeat from * around. Join.

**Round 4:** Ch 2, *dc in the next stitch, FPdc around the next 2 stitches.* Repeat from * around. Join.

Repeat Rounds 3 and 4 until the hat measures **20 (21, 22) cm** from the bottom of the brim.

> **What's happening?** In each round, the raised FPdc stitches sit on top of the flat dc stitches from the round before. This swapping is what creates the waffle squares.

## Step 3: Close the Top

Cut the yarn, leaving a 30 cm tail. Thread it onto your tapestry needle and weave it in and out of the stitches of the last round. Pull tightly to close the hole, then secure with a few stitches on the inside.

Add a pompom if you like, and weave in all the ends.

## Beginner Tips

- **Place a stitch marker** in the first stitch of each round so you always know where it starts.
- **Post stitches use more yarn**, so keep your tension relaxed.
- **Try the hat on** as you go – it's the easiest way to get the perfect length.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)

- **My waffle pattern looks like stripes.** Make sure you switch between Round 3 and Round 4 – don't repeat the same round twice.
- **The hat is too tight.** Waffle stitch is thick. Try a hook 0.5 mm bigger.
- **My stitch count keeps changing.** Remember the ch 2 at the start of each round does not count as a stitch.

## FAQ

### Is waffle stitch hard for beginners?

Not at all once you understand the front post double crochet. It's just two stitches repeated. Practice on a small swatch first if you're nervous.

### Can I make this beanie with thinner yarn?

Yes, but the hat will be smaller. Use more stitches in Round 1 (in multiples of 3) and check your gauge.

### How do I make the beanie slouchy?

Keep repeating Rounds 3 and 4 for another 4–6 cm before closing the top.
