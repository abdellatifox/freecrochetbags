## Skill Level: Easy
You should be familiar with basic crochet stitches, particularly half double crochet (hdc). This pattern mainly uses hdc, with some simple increases and decreases.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain  
- hdc – half double crochet  
- sl st – slip stitch  
- st(s) – stitch(es)  
- inc – increase (2 stitches in the same stitch)  
- dec – decrease (2 stitches together)  

## Size and Gauge
- Finished Size: Approximately 25 cm (10 in) in diameter and 20 cm (8 in) in height.
- Gauge: 12 hdc and 12 rows = 10 cm (4 in). To check gauge, make a swatch of 20 stitches in hdc for 10 rows.

## How to Crochet a Toilet Paper Storage Basket (Step by Step)

### Base of the Basket
The base is worked in rounds.
1. **Foundation:** Ch 4, join with a sl st to form a ring. (1 st)
2. **Round 1:** Ch 2 (counts as hdc), work 9 hdc in the ring. Join with sl st to top of ch 2. (10 sts)
3. **Round 2:** Ch 2, (hdc, inc) in each st around. Join with sl st. (20 sts)
4. **Round 3:** Ch 2, (hdc in next st, inc) around. Join with sl st. (30 sts)
5. **Round 4:** Ch 2, (hdc in next 2 sts, inc) around. Join with sl st. (40 sts)
6. **Round 5:** Ch 2, (hdc in next 3 sts, inc) around. Join with sl st. (50 sts)
7. **Round 6:** Ch 2, (hdc in next 4 sts, inc) around. Join with sl st. (60 sts)

### Body of the Basket
The body is also worked in rounds, continuing from the base.
8. **Round 7:** Ch 2, hdc in each st around. Join with sl st. (60 sts)
9. **Repeat Round 7** for an additional 9 rounds, totaling 10 rounds for the body.

### Turning the Basket Edges
We’ll start to shape the basket now.
10. **Round 17:** Ch 2, (hdc in next 4 sts, dec) around. Join with sl st. (50 sts)
11. **Round 18:** Ch 2, (hdc in next 3 sts, dec) around. Join with sl st. (40 sts)
12. **Round 19:** Ch 2, (hdc in next 2 sts, dec) around. Join with sl st. (30 sts)
13. **Round 20:** Ch 2, (hdc in next st, dec) around. Join with sl st. (20 sts)
14. **Round 21:** Ch 2, dec around. Join with sl st. (10 sts)

### Finishing the Basket
15. **Final Round:** Ch 1, sc in each st around to create a finished edge. Join with sl st. (10 sts)
16. Fasten off and weave in all ends.

## Beginner Tips
- Keep your yarn organized to avoid tangling.
- Check your basket's height as you work to ensure it matches the desired look.
- Maintain even tension on your stitches for a neat finish.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My basket is too small." – This could happen if your gauge is off. Check it and adjust your hook size accordingly.
- "The stitches are uneven." – This often means uneven tension. Practice keeping a consistent tension as you crochet.
- "The edges aren’t smooth." – This may be due to fasten-off method. Make sure to follow the final round carefully for a clean edge.

## How to Care for Your Toilet Paper Storage Basket
To keep your storage basket looking fresh, hand wash or machine wash it on a gentle cycle. Lay it flat to dry to help it maintain its shape, especially if you used fabric stiffener.

## FAQ
### Can I customize the size?
Yes! If you want a larger basket, simply add more rounds to the body while increasing stitches as needed.

### What yarn can I use instead?
You can substitute any worsted weight yarn, but make sure it’s durable since the basket will hold items.

### How long does it take to make?
Expect to spend about 3 hours total, broken down into about 30 minutes for the base, 2 hours for the body, and 30 minutes for finishing.
