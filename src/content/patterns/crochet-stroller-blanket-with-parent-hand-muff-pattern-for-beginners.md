This crochet stroller blanket pattern makes a beautiful and functional item for parents pushing strollers. The integrated hand muff keeps your hands warm while you provide comfort and warmth for your little one. Using simple stitches, this pattern is straightforward and enjoyable, making it perfect for crochet newcomers.

You’ll learn to work with double crochet and create a seamless join for the muff, making this project great for anyone looking to expand their crochet skills while crafting something useful.

## Why You'll Love This Stroller Blanket
- Cozy and practical – perfect for keeping both baby and parent warm while on the go.
- Quick to make – you can complete the blanket in just 6-8 hours and the muff in 2-3 hours.
- Easy color changes – the pattern allows for creativity with colors, from muted tones to bright, playful shades.
- Functional design – the hand muff is conveniently integrated, making it a unique and thoughtful gift.

## Skill Level: Easy
This pattern is perfect for beginners! You’ll be using basic stitches like double crochet and single crochet, making it simple to follow along. 

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain  
- dc – double crochet  
- st – stitch  
- sk – skip  
- sl st – slip stitch  
- sc – single crochet  

## Size and Gauge
- Finished Blanket: 91.5 × 61 cm (36 × 24 in)  
- Finished Muff: 30.5 × 17.75 cm (12 × 7 in)  
- Gauge: 16 dc and 20 rows = 10 cm (4 in)  

Make sure to check your gauge by making a swatch. If your measurements don’t match, adjust your hook size accordingly.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Part 1: Blanket
The blanket is worked in rows.  
1. **Foundation Chain:** Ch 108.  
2. **Row 1:** Dc in the 4th ch from hook (counts as 1 dc), dc in each ch across. (106 dc)  
3. **Row 2:** Ch 3, turn. Dc in each st across. (106 dc)  
4. **Repeat Row 2** until the piece measures 61 cm (24 in) in length (approx. 12 rows).  
5. **Color Change:** Change to Color B.  
6. **Row 14-36:** With Color B, repeat Row 2 until the blanket is 91.5 cm (36 in) long.  
7. **Border:** 
   - Ch 1, sc evenly around the blanket, placing 3 sc in each corner. Join with a sl st to the first sc. Fasten off.  

### Part 2: Hand Muff
The muff is worked in rows and then folded.  
1. **Foundation Chain:** Ch 37.  
2. **Row 1:** Dc in the 4th ch from hook, dc in each ch across. (35 dc)  
3. **Row 2:** Ch 3, turn. Dc in each st across. (35 dc)  
4. **Repeat Row 2** until the muff measures 30.5 cm (12 in) in length (approx. 7 rows).  
5. **Fold and Join:** Fold the muff in half widthwise. 
   - Sl st across the open sides to close the muff. Turn inside out for a neat finish.
6. **Bottom Edge:** With Color A, sc around the bottom of the muff (12 sc on either side), joining with a sl st. Fasten off.  

## How to Join Your Muff to Blanket
1. Position the muff on the blanket at one end, centered.  
2. Use a whip stitch to attach the top edge of the muff to the blanket securely. Ensure the muff can still be used comfortably.

## Finishing Tip: Weave Ends Neatly
Use a tapestry needle to weave in all yarn ends. 

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Stroller Blanket
- Keep your workspace organized to easily find your supplies.
- Maintain an even tension to ensure your stitches look uniform.
- Measure your work periodically to check sizes against the pattern.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My blanket is curling at the edges." – This could happen if you're working your stitches too tightly. Try to relax your tension.
- "The muff is too small." – Make sure you followed the foundation chain and counted your stitches accurately.
- "My color changes look messy." – Use a stitch marker to indicate the beginning of each row to keep your transitions clean.

## How to Care for Your Stroller Blanket
Machine wash on a gentle cycle and tumble dry on low. Avoid bleach to maintain the quality of the yarn.
