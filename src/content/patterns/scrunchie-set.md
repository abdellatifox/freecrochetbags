Got a basket of leftover yarn? This **easy crochet scrunchie pattern** turns small scraps into soft, ruffled hair ties in about 20 minutes. They're gentle on hair, fun to make, and perfect for gifts or selling at craft fairs.

You work right over a plain hair elastic, so there's no sewing at all.

## Why You'll Love These Crochet Scrunchies

- **Ready in 20 minutes**.
- **Uses up scraps** – about 25 m of yarn each.
- **No sewing** – you crochet directly onto the elastic.
- **Three styles** from one simple pattern.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)

- **ch** – chain
- **sl st** – slip stitch
- **sc** – single crochet
- **dc** – double crochet

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Round 1: Cover the Elastic

Make a slip knot on your hook. Hold the hair elastic in your left hand. Insert your hook **through the center of the elastic**, yarn over and pull up a loop, yarn over and pull through both loops – that's your first sc worked around the elastic.

Work **60 sc** around the elastic, pushing the stitches together as you go so they fit. Join with a sl st to the first sc. **(60 sc)**

### Round 2: Make the Ruffle

Ch 3 (counts as a dc), 2 dc in the same stitch. Work **3 dc in each stitch** around. Join to the top of the ch-3. **(180 dc)**

> **What's happening?** By putting three stitches into every stitch, you create too much fabric for the circle – so it twists and ruffles. That's the "scrunch"!

### Round 3: Finish the Edge

Ch 1, sc in each stitch around. Join and fasten off. Weave in the ends.

## Three Easy Variations

- **Two-tone:** work Round 3 in a contrasting color.
- **Skinny scrunchie:** work Round 2 in hdc and skip Round 3.
- **Softer ruffle:** work 2 dc (instead of 3) in each stitch in Round 2 for a gentler, less twisty look.

## Beginner Tips

- **Use a smooth yarn** – it's easier to see your stitches.
- **Don't stretch the elastic** while crocheting; let the stitches bunch up naturally.
- **Cotton and bamboo** are the gentlest yarns for hair.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)

- **I can't fit 60 stitches.** Push the stitches tightly together, or use 50 stitches for thicker yarn.
- **The scrunchie is too floppy.** Use a smaller hook for Round 2.
- **The elastic is showing.** Add a few more stitches in Round 1.

## FAQ

### What yarn is best for crochet scrunchies?

Soft cotton, bamboo or a silky acrylic. Avoid wool, which can feel scratchy and may felt.

### Can I sell scrunchies made from this pattern?

Yes! You're welcome to sell items you make. Please don't share or sell the written pattern itself.

### How long does it take to crochet a scrunchie?

About 15–20 minutes once you're comfortable with the pattern.
